# Data Processing Agreement (DPA) — v1.0.0 (en-US)

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement
between the customer ("Controller") and HuGR Labs ("Processor")
governing the processing of personal data through the CoreLink service.

## 1. Subject matter

Processor processes personal data on behalf of Controller solely to
deliver the CoreLink shared content-addressable cache service.

## 2. Duration

The DPA applies for the term of the underlying agreement.

## 3. Nature and purpose of processing

Storage, retrieval, deduplication, and audit logging of customer
content. No secondary use.

## 4. Categories of data

Object metadata, access tokens, audit events. End-user PII only as
explicitly placed by Controller.

## 5. Sub-processors

Cloudflare (Workers, R2, D1), Stripe (billing). See `legal/sub-processors.md`.

## 6. Security measures

Processor implements the technical and organisational measures detailed
in `specs/03_architecture/canonical/security_model.md` and the SOC 2 +
ISO 27001 control set referenced therein.

## 7. Controller rights

Controller may audit Processor's compliance through documented evidence
(SOC 2 report, audit log exports) on reasonable notice.

## 8. Data subject rights

Processor assists Controller in fulfilling data subject requests via
the DSR pipeline (S-11).

## 9. Breach notification

Processor notifies Controller without undue delay and in any case within
72 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach.

## 10. Sub-processor changes

Processor maintains a public sub-processor registry and notifies
Controller at least 30 days before adding a new sub-processor.

## 11. International transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA / UK, the EU
Standard Contractual Clauses ("SCCs") form an integral part of this
DPA.

## 12. Return or deletion

On termination, Processor returns or deletes personal data per
Controller's instructions, subject to legal retention obligations.

## 13. Governing law

The governing law of the underlying agreement applies.
