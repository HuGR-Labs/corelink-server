## §0 — Selection rubric (mirrored from engagement-letter §3)

Scoring is **out of 100** across 9 dimensions. Minimum acceptance threshold: **≥ 70/100 weighted**.

### §0.1 Primary capability gates (each 0-10; weighted ×7 = 70 max)

| # | Dimension | Weight | What is scored |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | LFPDPPP statutory + Reglamento depth | 10 | Authored ≥ 1 peer-reviewed LFPDPPP article OR published INAI PPD/verificación opinion |
| 2 | INAI procedural experience | 10 | ≥ 3 ARCO petitions OR ≥ 1 verificación procedure filed |
| 3 | Cross-border SaaS familiarity | 10 | ≥ 1 cross-border SaaS privacy notice review in last 3 years |
| 4 | Spanish-native + English-bilingual | 10 | Opinion deliverable in MX Spanish formal-legal register; bilingual for cross-team |
| 5 | Written-output discipline | 10 | Delivers redlined documents + structured opinion (not verbal Q&A) |
| 6 | Cédula profesional + BAR registration | 10 | Active Mexican cédula profesional; member of Barra Mexicana or equivalent |
| 7 | EVT-044 PDF sign-off readiness | 10 | Willing to sign EVT-044 PDFs with cédula + BLAKE3/SHA-256 hash attestation |

### §0.2 Secondary dimensions (each 0-10; weighted ×3 = 30 max)

| # | Dimension | Weight | What is scored |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | Cost fit within USD 2,500-5,400 envelope | 10 | Indicative quote within target band |
| 9 | Timeline fit (2026-05-20 → 2026-06-20 window) | 10 | Ability to deliver written opinion within 21 days of retainer signature |

**Total max:** 100. **Minimum:** 70. Final selection per weighted score + Owner judgment.

## §3 — Selection methodology + Owner decision flow

### §3.1 Recommended decision flow

1. **Send tier-1 (3 firms) in parallel** (OLIVARES, Basham, Sánchez Devanny) using the engagement-email template + scoping packet + engagement letter. **14 calendar days response window** per §3.2 below.
2. **Within 14 days** — receive responses; expect 1-3 written proposals + 0-2 conflict-decline replies. Update tracker JSON to `RESPONSE_RECEIVED`.
3. **7-day vetting window** — reference-check (1-2 prior LFPDPPP clients per firm, NDA-permitting) + cédula profesional verification via SEP RNPC + Chambers/Legal500 cross-check. Update tracker to `IN_NEGOTIATION` for top candidate.
4. **Select 1 firm** — preference order: best weighted-score match within cost envelope + soonest turnaround commit + cleanest conflict posture. Sign retainer per `docs/legal/lfpdppp-mx-retainer-template.md`. Update tracker to `RETAINER_SIGNED`; pay 50% deposit.
5. **If all 3 tier-1 decline or out-of-bandwidth** — escalate to tier-2 (Galicia → Creel). Same 14-day response window.

### §3.2 RFP response deadline

Send date + 14 calendar days = response deadline. For wave-28 send window:

- **Send target:** 2026-05-20 (T+0)
- **Response deadline:** 2026-06-03 (T+14)
- **Selection decision:** 2026-06-10 (T+21)
- **Retainer signature target:** 2026-06-12 (T+23)
- **Opinion delivery target:** 2026-07-03 (T+44 from send; T+21 from retainer)

### §3.3 Conflict-of-interest pre-flight checklist

For each candidate, Owner must verify before sending:

- [ ] No current / recent (≤ 3 years) representation of Cloudflare, Inc. or any Cloudflare subsidiary.
- [ ] No current / recent (≤ 3 years) representation of Stripe, Inc.
- [ ] No current / recent (≤ 3 years) representation of Neon, Inc.
- [ ] No current / recent (≤ 3 years) representation of Grafana Labs.
- [ ] No representation of a direct CoreLink competitor (multi-tenant content-addressable cache / build-cache / package-cache SaaS).
- [ ] No representation of any HuGR Labs cap-table participant in pending dispute.
- [ ] Standard firm conflict-check completed and cleared at intake.

If any of the above flag, request a written conflict waiver before proceeding OR move to the next candidate.

### §3.4 Cédula profesional verification

For the **selected** attorney (post-retainer-signature), Owner must verify the active cédula profesional via:

- https://www.cedulaprofesional.sep.gob.mx/cedula/ — search by attorney full name.
- Cross-check cédula number against the cédula displayed in the firm's website team bio.
- Record the cédula number in `legal/privacy-notice/v1.0.0/metadata.yaml.legal_review.mx_attorney_cedula` field (per `legal/privacy-notice/REVIEW_PROCESS.md §2.3`).
