# SLA and Terms capability correction package — v1.0.1 draft

This draft is the proposed **contract-amendment** path for B-089 and the
BYOK/SLA portion of B-154. It does not alter `legal/sla/v1.0.0.md`, the
published Terms, any customer agreement, or billing behavior.

## Proposed sold-tier matrix

The public rate card has six tiers: Free, Solo, Starter, Pro, Max, and
Enterprise. The current four-tier SLA taxonomy and the Terms' Pro credit
promise do not match that rate card or the absence of a credit-issuance path.

| Tier | SLA / credit posture in this draft |
|---|---|
| Free, Solo, Starter, Pro, Max | No automatic service credit promise. |
| Enterprise | A credit remedy exists only if stated in a separately executed order form; no automatic issuance is promised by this base document. |

## Proposed replacement clauses

Replace the current automatic-issuance sentence with:

> This base SLA does not promise automatic service-credit issuance. Any
> negotiated credit remedy, if applicable, must be stated in a separately
> executed order form that identifies the tier, measurement window, credit
> amount, and manual or automated settlement process.

Replace the Enterprise BYOK kill-switch SLO with:

> BYOK and its kill-switch are not enabled or provisioned in the launched
> service. No BYOK kill-switch latency SLO or credit is included in this base
> SLA.

Replace the Terms' §14 Pro paragraph with:

> CoreLink does not promise automatic service credits in these base Terms.
> Any availability credit must be stated in a separately executed order form
> or amendment; no credit is issued unless that instrument and the billing
> process are both effective.

## Approval gate

Counsel and Finance must approve this path, decide the treatment of existing
customers, and provide a signed version/reference before publication. The
repository has no authority to issue credits or to treat this draft as a
remedy. B-089 and B-154 remain open until the executed choice is evidenced.
