# B-170 owner artifact templates

These are unsigned, unsubmitted templates for the three external records required
by [B-170](https://github.com/HuGR-dev/corelink-server/issues/1677). They help the
owner capture a decision and its source without putting contract bytes, credentials,
or recipient personal data in the repository.

The templates are not the canonical evidence records. Complete records belong at
the paths named by `docs/internal/b087-questionnaire-owner-actions.md`:

- `reports/owner-actions/b170-legal-contract-review.md`
- `reports/owner-actions/b170-pagerduty-export.json`
- `reports/owner-actions/b170-recipient-notification-decision.md`

Do not copy a template into one of those paths until the responsible owner has
provided and reviewed the external evidence. Template presence does not change the
B-170 state, and does not prove approval, execution, export, notification, delivery,
or receipt.
