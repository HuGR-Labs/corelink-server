This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") supplements the CoreLink Master
Subscription Agreement ("MSA") between the customer ("Controller") and
CoreLink ("Processor") and governs the processing of Personal Data by
CoreLink on behalf of the Controller.

## 1. Subject matter and duration

CoreLink processes Personal Data only on the documented instructions of the
Controller, for the duration of the MSA, and for the purpose of providing the
Service.

## 2. Nature and purpose of processing

The categories of Personal Data, types of data subjects, and processing
operations are described in **Annex I** of this DPA.

## 3. Sub-processors

The Controller authorizes CoreLink to engage the Sub-processors listed at
`/privacy/sub-processors`. CoreLink will notify the Controller at least 30
days before adding or replacing any Sub-processor; the Controller may object
on reasonable grounds.

## 4. Security measures

CoreLink implements the technical and organizational measures set out in
**Annex II**, including encryption in transit and at rest, access control,
audit logging (7-year retention), incident response procedures, and annual
penetration testing.

## 5. Data subject rights

CoreLink assists the Controller in fulfilling the obligations under GDPR
Articles 15–22 and LGPD Articles 18–22 via the DSR API and the Admin UI.

## 6. Personal data breach notification

CoreLink notifies the Controller without undue delay, and in any case within
**72 hours**, after becoming aware of a Personal Data breach.

## 7. International transfers

Transfers outside the EEA rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses
(Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914) and the EU-US Data Privacy
Framework, as applicable.

## 8. Audit

The Controller has the right to audit CoreLink's compliance with this DPA
once per calendar year on 30 days' prior notice, or more frequently if
required by a Supervisory Authority.
