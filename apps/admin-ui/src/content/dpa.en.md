# Data Processing Agreement (DPA) — v1.0.0

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") supplements the CoreLink Master
Subscription Agreement and applies whenever CoreLink processes personal
data on behalf of the Customer.

## 1. Roles

The Customer is the data controller. CoreLink Labs, Inc. is the data
processor. Each Party complies with applicable data protection laws,
including the GDPR, LGPD, and the CCPA.

## 2. Scope of processing

CoreLink processes personal data only to provide the contracted services:
content-addressable cache storage, build artifact distribution, and
related operational telemetry. We do not sell personal data.

## 3. Sub-processors

CoreLink maintains an up-to-date list of sub-processors at
`/legal/sub-processors`. We notify Customer of additions at least 30 days
in advance and accept written objection within that window.

## 4. Security

CoreLink applies organizational and technical measures appropriate to the
risk, including encryption in transit and at rest, scoped credentials,
audit logging, and regular third-party security testing.

## 5. Data subject rights

CoreLink assists the Customer in responding to data subject access,
rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, and objection
requests within the timelines required by applicable law.

## 6. International transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA or the United Kingdom,
the Parties incorporate the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2)
by reference, supplemented by the UK Addendum where required.

## 7. Breach notification

CoreLink notifies the Customer without undue delay, and in any event
within 72 hours, of any confirmed personal data breach.

## 8. Audits

The Customer may, no more than once per year and with reasonable notice,
request a SOC 2 Type II report or commission an independent audit at
Customer expense.

## 9. Term and termination

This DPA remains in force for as long as CoreLink processes personal
data on the Customer's behalf. On termination, CoreLink deletes or
returns personal data within 30 days unless retention is required by law.

## 10. Acceptance

By clicking "I accept" the authorized representative of the Customer
acknowledges they have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by this
DPA on behalf of the Customer organization.
