# Pricing overview

**Understanding-oriented.** This page explains *what* each CoreLink tier
includes, *why* the tier boundaries are where they are, and *how* the
bill is computed. For interactive estimation, see the
[cost calculator](./calculator). For head-to-head comparisons against
self-hosted alternatives, see [vs. alternatives](./comparison).

> **DRAFT — pending Finance + Marketing + Product sign-off.** List prices
> below are launch-day targets and remain placeholder-eligible until the
> Finance audit trail is closed. Customers running pre-GA pilots should
> consider the numbers indicative, not contractual.

## The four tiers

CoreLink ships four customer-facing tiers. Three are self-serve via
Stripe Checkout (Starter, Team); one is instant-activation (Free); one
routes through an inquiry form (Enterprise).

| Tier | Monthly price | Included CAS storage | Included read traffic | BYOK | Region pin | Activation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | 5 GB | 50 GB / mo | — | shared | Instant |
| **Starter** | $29 | 50 GB | 500 GB / mo | — | 1 region | Stripe Checkout |
| **Team** | $199 | 500 GB | 5 TB / mo | optional add-on | up to 2 regions | Stripe Checkout |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom | Custom | included | all 4 regions | Inquiry form |

All tiers run on the same data plane, the same SLO catalog, and the
same audit and residency model. The differences are quotas, BYOK
inclusion, region count, and support tier — not capability.

## How the bill is computed

The monthly invoice for paid tiers is:

```
invoice = base_fee
        + max(0, observed_storage_gb - included_storage_gb) × overage_storage_per_gb
        + max(0, observed_reads_gb   - included_reads_gb)   × overage_reads_per_gb
        + optional_add_ons
```

Overage unit prices:

| Tier | Storage overage ($/GB/mo) | Read overage ($/GB) |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $0.20 | $0.04 |
| Team | $0.15 | $0.03 |
| Enterprise | negotiated | negotiated |

Free tier has no overage line: it is hard-capped at the included quotas
and returns `429 Too Many Requests` past the cap until the next
calendar month. The cap is a feature, not a punishment — it is what
keeps Free free.

BYOK add-on for Team is a flat $99/month and provides per-tenant DEK
wrapping by a customer-controlled KEK in AWS KMS, GCP KMS, or Azure
Key Vault.

## Why no per-egress line item

Cloud egress is, on most managed cache vendors, the dominant line item
on a heavy-CI bill. CoreLink stores cache content on Cloudflare R2,
which charges zero for egress on reads to the public internet and to
Cloudflare's network. CoreLink passes that property through to
customers: paid tiers price storage and per-GB *read* operations (a
per-operation amortization), not per-GB *bytes* leaving the cache.

This is structurally why the CoreLink bill at heavy read volume is
typically a multiple smaller than the S3-backed self-hosted equivalent
at the same hit-rate and working-set. The [comparison page](./comparison)
walks the arithmetic at three scale points.

## Why no per-seat line item on Starter / Team

CoreLink does not charge per developer seat. Cache load correlates with
build volume and CI parallelism, not with seat count, and per-seat
pricing creates a perverse incentive against onboarding the next
engineer to the cache. Seats are capped per tier (above) as an
anti-abuse measure; they are not a billing dimension.

Enterprise removes the cap entirely.

## Why no compute / CPU line item

CoreLink is a cache, not a build runner. Customers run their own
runners (GitHub Actions, BuildJet, self-hosted, etc.); CoreLink serves
the cache. There is therefore no per-minute compute line item on the
CoreLink invoice — the customer is already paying their CI vendor for
compute.

## What the Free tier is for

The Free tier is designed for two cases:

1. **Solo developers** running personal monorepos. 5 GB and 50 GB/mo of
   reads is enough for a typical personal Bazel build with daily
   incremental green builds.
2. **Evaluation by larger teams** who want to wire CoreLink into a
   single CI pipeline alongside their existing cache for a two-week
   shadow comparison.

Free is not a trial that expires. It is a permanent free tier with
hard quotas. Most teams whose CI volume justifies a managed cache
graduate to Starter or Team within a week of seriously using it.

## What Enterprise gives you that Team does not

- **BYOK inclusion** (Team can buy it as an add-on; Enterprise has it).
- **All four regions** simultaneously, not just two.
- **Negotiated DPA** rather than clickwrap, including custom data-handling
  language for regulated industries (financial services, healthcare,
  defense-adjacent).
- **Custom retention** for audit logs and compliance reports — multi-year
  retention is available on negotiated terms.
- **Dedicated support** with a 1-hour SLO during business hours, with
  named technical account manager assignments above a usage threshold.
- **Custom quotas** for storage, reads, and seats.

Enterprise is the right fit when at least one of the following is
true: residency requirements exceed two regions; security review
requires BYOK; the customer's auditors require multi-year retention;
the customer's CI fleet exceeds Team's quotas during peak.

### Caveat: 4 customer-facing tiers vs. 5 internal tiers

The internal billing system models five tiers — Free, Starter, Team,
Pro, Enterprise — to support an interim Pro SKU that Finance may or
may not enable for GA-day depending on the customer mix at the time.
The pages here describe the four customer-facing tiers that will be
listed on the public marketing site at GA-day. If the Pro SKU is
enabled before GA, this page and the calculator will be updated to
include it before the public toggle flips.
