# Leave CoreLink — offboarding in 5 steps

This guide walks you through cancelling your CoreLink tenant.
We designed the flow so leaving is **as friction-free as joining**,
with three guarantees:

1. **Your data is portable.** From T+0 to T+30 you can run a single
   CLI command and walk away with a content-addressed archive of
   every blob, every audit row, and every RBAC artifact.
2. **You can change your mind.** From T+0 to T+45 you can revert
   to a fully-active tenant with one click. No support ticket
   required.
3. **At T+90 we cryptographically erase everything.** When BYOK
   is enabled, we destroy the customer-managed encryption key —
   meaning even our own engineers can't recover the data after that
   point. You get a signed receipt.

For the legal posture (refund mechanics, MSA §10, DPA v1.0.0 §11),
see the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) — §3 (fees and refunds) and
§10 (term and termination) — and our [Trust page](/trust/data-handling).

:::note Individual data-subject rights are a different flow
If you are a **natural person** whose data we process and you want
to exercise your individual rights under LGPD Art. 18 / GDPR
Art. 15-22 / CCPA §1798.105 — use the DSR self-service surface at
`/privacy/dsr`, not this guide. This guide is for **whole-tenant
commercial offboarding**.
:::

## The 5-state lifecycle

When you cancel, your tenant moves through 5 canonical states:

| State | When | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| `CANCEL_REQUESTED` | T+0 | Everything (read / write / admin / export / revert). |
| `GRACE_PERIOD` | T+1 to T+30 | Everything. **Export your data here.** |
| `READ_ONLY` | T+30 to T+45 | Read + export + revert. Writes return HTTP 409. |
| `SUSPENDED` | T+45 to T+90 | Nothing self-service. Contact support if you want back in. |
| `ERASED` | T+90+ | Terminal. Cryptographic erasure committed. |

## Step 1 — Initiate cancellation (T+0)

In the dashboard, go to **Settings → Tenant → Cancel my tenant**.

You will be asked to:

- Confirm your account email.
- Pass a fresh MFA step-up challenge (anti-fraud).
- Optionally fill the cancellation survey (free-form text;
  ≤ 4 KB; sent to product, not used against you in support).

If you prefer the API:

```bash
curl -X POST https://corelink-api.humangr.com/v1/tenant/$TENANT_ID/offboard \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CORELINK_TOKEN" \
  -H "X-Corelink-MFA-Step-Up: $STEP_UP_TOKEN" \
  -d '{"reason": "switching to in-house solution"}'
```

The response contains your `cancel_requested_at_ms` anchor —
this is your **T+0 timestamp**; every subsequent deadline is
computed from this.

You will receive a confirmation email within 60 seconds. If you
**do not** receive it, your cancellation **did not land** — try
again or email support (the system is designed to fail-CLOSED,
so a missing email means a missing audit row).

## Step 2 — Export your data (T+0..T+30)

You have 30 days to export everything. The canonical CLI command:

```bash
corelink tenant export \
  --tenant-id $TENANT_ID \
  --output ./corelink-backup-$(date +%Y%m%d).tar.zst
```

The output archive contains:

- **CAS blobs.** Every BLAKE3-addressed blob stored under your
  tenant prefix. Content-addressed, so you can verify integrity
  byte-by-byte against the digests in the manifest.
- **Audit-chain slice.** Every audit row attributable to your
  tenant. Merkle-linked + RFC 6962 inclusion proofs included
  (so you can present this as evidence to your next vendor or
  your auditor without us in the loop).
- **RBAC roster.** Every user, every role, every API token
  (rotated / revoked status preserved).
- **DPA acceptance receipt.** Signed JWT of the DPA version you
  accepted, with the canonical `corelink-dpa-acceptance`
  envelope.

Verification:

```bash
corelink tenant verify-export \
  --archive ./corelink-backup-$(date +%Y%m%d).tar.zst
```

The export endpoint remains reachable through T+45 (READ_ONLY),
but we recommend exporting in the T+0..T+30 window so you have
buffer time if something goes wrong.

## Step 3 — Change your mind (T+0..T+45)

You can revert to ACTIVE at any time during the first 45 days,
with full data:

```bash
curl -X POST https://corelink-api.humangr.com/v1/tenant/$TENANT_ID/offboard/revert \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CORELINK_TOKEN"
```

Or click **Settings → Tenant → Restore my tenant** in the
dashboard. There is no penalty, no support call, no escalation.
Your tenant immediately returns to a fully-active state.

After T+45 (SUSPENDED), restoration requires a support call;
email `support@humangr.com` and we'll process a force-revert
within one business day. After T+90 (ERASED), restoration is no
longer possible — the data is cryptographically gone.

## Step 4 — Watch the timeline (T+30, T+45)

You will receive 3 transactional emails between T+0 and T+45:

- **T+1 — "Your CoreLink tenant entered the grace period."**
  Tells you the export command + the revert URL.
- **T+30 — "Your CoreLink tenant is now read-only."**
  Writes will start returning HTTP 409. You can still read +
  export + revert.
- **T+45 — "Your CoreLink tenant has been suspended."**
  Last-chance email. Self-service revert is no longer available
  after this point; contact support if you change your mind.

If you stop receiving emails, check that
`@humangr.com` is not being blocked by your provider. The
audit chain is the authoritative record of every notification
that was attempted.

## Step 5 — Receive the erasure receipt (T+90)

At T+90, our operations team commits the final erasure with a
dry-run preview (two-operator dual-approval; auditable). When the
cascade completes you will receive:

- A signed `corelink.tenant.offboarding.erased` envelope (JCS-
  canonicalized; RS256-signed) listing the canonical erased
  artifacts (blob count, audit row count, BYOK CMK destroy
  receipt when applicable).
- An `Ed25519` erasure attestation from `corelink-erasure-
  attestation` (the canonical 7-year proof you can present to
  your auditor; mirrors the existing per-record DSR erasure
  attestation surface).

**If you have BYOK enabled:** the customer-managed CMK in your
KMS (AWS / GCP / Azure / Vault) is destroyed. This means even
our own engineers cannot recover the data from this point on,
because the envelope-encryption DEK that wrapped your blobs is
mathematically unrecoverable.

**If you do NOT have BYOK enabled:** we rely on our internal
key rotation + R2/D1/KV tombstone-and-purge cascade. Same
forensic outcome; weaker mathematical guarantee. (This is why
we offer BYOK for free on the Enterprise tier — when you leave,
you take the only copy of the key with you.)

## What to expect about timing

- **T+0**: cancellation confirmed; full capabilities retained.
- **T+1**: grace period starts (no functional change yet).
- **T+30**: writes start failing with HTTP 409 (`offboarding_read_only`).
- **T+45**: full suspension; reads start failing with HTTP 410 (`offboarding_suspended`).
- **T+90**: final erasure committed; receipts emailed.

Edge cases:

- **Force-advance** (skip ahead): if your jurisdiction issues a
  takedown order, we may force-advance with an audit trail.
  This is rare and we will contact you BEFORE acting unless
  legally prohibited.
- **Legal hold**: if your tenant is under a litigation hold,
  the timer pauses. You will be notified; the hold is logged
  in the audit chain.
