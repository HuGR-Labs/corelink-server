# Responsible disclosure

> **DRAFT — pending Legal + Finance + Segurança review.** The reward amounts
> and PGP key fingerprint below are placeholders until the program goes live.

We believe coordinated disclosure protects users. If you have found a
vulnerability in CoreLink, please contact us before discussing it publicly.

## How to report

- **Email:** `security@humangr.com`
- **PGP fingerprint:** `TBD` (key to be published on the trust portal)
- **Subject prefix:** `[VULN]`

Include, where possible:

- A clear description of the issue and its impact.
- Steps to reproduce, ideally as a self-contained script.
- Your assessment of severity (CVSS or qualitative).
- Whether you intend to disclose publicly and on what timeline.

## What we commit to

| Stage | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledge receipt | within TBD business days |
| Initial triage | within TBD business days |
| Status update cadence | every TBD business days |
| Fix or mitigation | severity-dependent |
| Public credit (if desired) | at coordinated disclosure |

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who follow this policy in
good faith.

## Out of scope

- Denial-of-service attacks against production systems.
- Social engineering of CoreLink staff.
- Issues exclusively affecting outdated browsers or unsupported clients.
