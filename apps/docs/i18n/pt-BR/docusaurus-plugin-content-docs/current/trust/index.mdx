# Trust Center

CoreLink is a shared, content-addressable build cache that sees customer build
artefacts, signed audit events, and (optionally) customer-managed key material.
This Trust Center is the **single public-facing index** of how we secure that
surface, who we share data with, and what we are willing to commit to in
writing.

> Looking for something specific? Auditors and procurement teams typically
> jump straight to [Compliance](./compliance), [Sub-processors](./subprocessors),
> or [Data handling](./data-handling). Customers running a vendor-risk review
> usually want all three plus [Incident response](./incident-response).

## Posture at a glance

CoreLink is engineered for a **cross-tenant blast radius of zero**. That
shapes everything else on this page. The headline commitments are:

1. **Tenant isolation is invariant-bound.** Tenant A cannot read, write, or
   enumerate Tenant B's blobs. The invariant (`INV-TenantIsolation`) is
   TLA+ model-checked and gated by CI.
2. **Every artefact is hash-pinned.** No blob ever served diverges from the
   requested digest (`INV-CAS-INTEGRITY`).
3. **Every write produces an auditable, signed event.** The audit log is
   append-only, Merkle-linked, and exportable as RFC 6962 inclusion proofs
   that you can verify offline.
4. **You can hold your own keys.** Enterprise tenants can wrap their data
   encryption keys with their own KMS root — we never see plaintext key
   material. Four KMS providers are supported on day one.
5. **We commit to a 72-hour breach-notification window.** Aligned with
   LGPD Art. 33 and GDPR Art. 33; communicated through the channels you
   pre-register at onboarding.

## What's verifiable vs. what's attested

Compliance docs can be theatre. We try hard to make ours falsifiable:

- **Verifiable by you, right now:**
  audit-chain proofs ([how-to](/security/audit-chain)), the
  [security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt) contact card, the
  [SBOM](https://github.com/HumanGuardrail/corelink/releases) shipped with every
  release, Sigstore / Rekor provenance entries for every binary, and our
  public [status page](https://status.corelink.humangr.com).
- **Attested by us (with evidence on request):**
  SOC 2 readiness rollup, BYOK FIPS attestation matrix per provider, vendor
  risk register, LGPD Art. 33 residency attestation.
- **Attested by sub-processors (inherited):**
  Cloudflare, AWS, GCP, Azure, Clerk, Stripe, Drata SOC 2 Type II reports.

If you need the attested or inherited evidence packs, email
**trust@humangr.com** with your DPA on file; we route DPA-gated NDA bundles
back within 1 business day.
