# Pricing vs. alternatives

**Understanding-oriented.** This page walks the total-cost-of-ownership
arithmetic for three alternatives to CoreLink at three scale points:
**10 GB/mo**, **100 GB/mo**, and **1 TB/mo** of monthly CAS write
volume. All numbers are best-faith estimates against published cloud
pricing and an explicit operational-tax model; sources are inline.

> **DRAFT — pending Engineering + Finance + Marketing sign-off.** The
> arithmetic is canonical; the cloud unit prices are public list prices
> as of 2026-05-15 and may shift; the operational-tax model is a
> defensible mid-point estimate, not a customer-specific quote.

## What we are comparing

| Vendor / pattern | Software | Storage | Egress | Operations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CoreLink** (this product) | managed | R2 (vendor) | zero (R2) | vendor |
| **bazel-remote self-host** | OSS (bazel-remote) | S3 | S3/CloudFront | customer |
| **S3 + CloudFront** | OSS (bazel-remote) or REAPI proxy | S3 | CloudFront | customer |
| **Harbor self-host** | OSS (Harbor) | S3 or local FS | depends | customer |

A note on Harbor: Harbor is a container registry, not strictly a Bazel
remote-cache backend. We include it because customers comparing
"managed cache vs. self-host" sometimes lump container registries
into the same RFP, and we want the comparison honest. Harbor as a
REAPI-conformant cache is an awkward fit; the comparison reflects that.

## Methodology

For each scale point we hold these constants:

| Constant | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Working-set retention | 3 months | typical Bazel CI working set |
| Hit rate | 80% | mid of typical observed range |
| Read multiplier vs. writes | 20× | each blob written is read ~20× by parallel CI |
| Region count | 1 | single-region baseline (multi-region is a CoreLink-favorable add) |
| Cloud unit prices | public, 2026-05 | see *Sources* per row |
| Engineer loaded cost | $200/hr | mid-market estimate, North America |

The variable inputs are:

- `S_w` = monthly CAS write volume (10 GB / 100 GB / 1 TB)
- Derived: `S_r` ≈ `S_w × 20 × 0.8` (read multiplier × hit rate)
- Derived: stored steady state ≈ `S_w × 3` (3-month retention)

Operational tax is modeled as engineer-hours per month spent on the
self-hosted cache:

| Scale | Ops-hours/month (estimated) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 10 GB/mo | 2 | weekly check-in, occasional GC tuning |
| 100 GB/mo | 8 | one full day per month: dashboards + eviction tuning + on-call |
| 1 TB/mo | 24 | three days per month: multi-region replication + capacity planning + incident response |

These are mid-point estimates from real customer interviews during
discovery; individual teams will vary by 0.5–2× depending on their
existing SRE culture. We do not claim these are precise; we claim they
are defensible.

## Scale 1: 10 GB / month write volume

A small team or solo developer.

- `S_w` = 10 GB/mo · `S_r` ≈ 160 GB/mo · stored ≈ 30 GB.

| Vendor | Storage $ | Egress $ | Software $ | Ops $ | **Total $/mo** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CoreLink Free** | included | $0 (R2) | $0 | $0 | **$0** |
| **CoreLink Starter** | included (under 50 GB) | $0 (R2) | $29 | $0 | **$29** |
| **bazel-remote on S3** | 30 GB × $0.023 = $0.69 | 160 GB × $0.09 = $14.40 | $0 (OSS) | 2 hr × $200 = $400 | **$415.09** |
| **S3 + CloudFront** | 30 GB × $0.023 = $0.69 | 160 GB × $0.085 = $13.60 | $0 (OSS) | 2 hr × $200 = $400 | **$414.29** |
| **Harbor self-host** | 30 GB × $0.023 = $0.69 | 160 GB × $0.09 = $14.40 | $0 (OSS) | 4 hr × $200 = $800 | **$815.09** (and not REAPI) |

Sources for cloud unit prices: S3 standard storage $0.023/GB; S3
egress $0.09/GB (first 10 TB); CloudFront NA/EU $0.085/GB (first 10
TB); R2 egress $0.00. All public list prices as of 2026-05.

**Takeaway at this scale.** The cloud unit-price line items are
trivial. The dominant line item is operations. CoreLink Free is $0;
any self-hosted alternative costs the customer at least one
engineer-touch per week. For a hobbyist, Free is the obvious choice;
for a tiny startup with one or two CI pipelines, even Starter at $29
is a rounding error compared to the engineer-hour cost.

Harbor is noted because the comparison gets asked. It is not a REAPI
remote cache; running Bazel against Harbor as a CAS backend requires
custom glue and is not recommended. We include it for transparency.

## Scale 2: 100 GB / month write volume

A 50–200 engineer team running a medium Bazel monorepo.

- `S_w` = 100 GB/mo · `S_r` ≈ 1,600 GB/mo · stored ≈ 300 GB.

| Vendor | Storage $ | Egress $ | Software $ | Ops $ | **Total $/mo** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CoreLink Starter** | included (50 GB) + overage (50 × $0.20 = $10) | $0 (R2) | $29 + ((1600−500) × $0.04 = $44) read overage | $0 | **$83.00** |
| **CoreLink Team** | included (500 GB) | $0 (R2) | $199 | $0 | **$199.00** |
| **bazel-remote on S3** | 300 GB × $0.023 = $6.90 | 1,600 GB × $0.09 = $144.00 | $0 (OSS) | 8 hr × $200 = $1,600 | **$1,750.90** |
| **S3 + CloudFront** | 300 GB × $0.023 = $6.90 | 1,600 GB × $0.085 = $136.00 | $0 (OSS) | 8 hr × $200 = $1,600 | **$1,742.90** |
| **Harbor self-host** | 300 GB × $0.023 = $6.90 | 1,600 GB × $0.09 = $144.00 | $0 (OSS) | 16 hr × $200 = $3,200 | **$3,350.90** (and not REAPI) |

Detail on CoreLink Starter at this scale:

```
base            = $29
storage_overage = max(0, 100 - 50)  × $0.20 = $10.00
read_overage    = max(0, 1600 - 500) × $0.04 = $44.00
total           = $29 + $10 + $44 = $83.00
```

So Starter-with-overage at this scale is **$83**, *cheaper* than Team's
flat $199. Why pick Team? Because at this scale the customer is one
month away from outgrowing Starter's quotas (the team is hiring, CI
volume is climbing) and Team's headroom is the prudent choice. The
calculator surfaces both lines so the customer decides — see
[calculator](./calculator) for the canonical rendering.

**Takeaway at this scale.** CoreLink Team at $199 lands roughly an
order of magnitude below the next-cheapest self-hosted alternative,
once operations are honestly priced in. Even **excluding** the
operational tax, the egress line item alone ($144/mo on S3) is most
of the way to CoreLink Team's flat $199. Adding ops makes the gap
overwhelming.

This is the scale at which "we'll just spin up our own cache" stops
being a defensible engineering decision and becomes an explicit
trade-off the team has to justify against budget pressure.

## Scale 3: 1 TB / month write volume

A large engineering org (500+ engineers), large Bazel or Buck2
monorepo, heavy CI, multiple product teams sharing the cache.

- `S_w` = 1,024 GB/mo · `S_r` ≈ 16,384 GB/mo · stored ≈ 3 TB.

| Vendor | Storage $ | Egress $ | Software $ | Ops $ | **Total $/mo** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CoreLink Team** | included 500 GB + overage (524 × $0.15 = $78.60) | $0 (R2) | $199 | $0 | **$615.52** (incl. $337.92 read overage) |
| **CoreLink Enterprise** | negotiated | $0 (R2) | quote | $0 | **quote** (typically lower per-GB than Team overage at this scale) |
| **bazel-remote on S3** | 3,072 GB × $0.023 = $70.66 | 16,384 GB × $0.09 = $1,474.56 | $0 (OSS) | 24 hr × $200 = $4,800 | **$6,345.22** |
| **S3 + CloudFront** | 3,072 GB × $0.023 = $70.66 | 16,384 GB × $0.085 = $1,392.64 | $0 (OSS) | 24 hr × $200 = $4,800 | **$6,263.30** |
| **Harbor self-host** | 3,072 GB × $0.023 = $70.66 | 16,384 GB × $0.09 = $1,474.56 | $0 (OSS) | 48 hr × $200 = $9,600 | **$11,145.22** (and not REAPI) |

Detail on CoreLink Team at this scale:

```
base            = $199
storage_overage = max(0, 1024 - 500)   × $0.15 = $78.60
read_overage    = max(0, 16384 - 5120) × $0.03 = $337.92
total           = $199 + $78.60 + $337.92 = $615.52
```

**Takeaway at this scale.** CoreLink Team at ~$616 is roughly an order
of magnitude below the self-hosted alternative at this scale point.
The egress line item alone on S3 ($1,474/mo) is more than 2× the
CoreLink Team total bill. Operations is another full engineer-week of
load per month.

At this scale, customers should also consider CoreLink Enterprise,
which typically prices below the Team-with-overage line for sustained
volumes above ~1.5 TB/mo of writes and adds BYOK + multi-region
(features that self-hosted alternatives would each be a separate
engineering project to implement).

## Side-by-side summary

| Scale | CoreLink | bazel-remote on S3 | S3 + CloudFront | Harbor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 GB/mo write | $0 (Free) or $29 | $415 | $414 | $815 |
| 100 GB/mo write | $83 (Starter+overage) or $199 (Team) | $1,751 | $1,743 | $3,351 |
| 1 TB/mo write | $615 (Team) or quote (Ent.) | $6,345 | $6,263 | $11,145 |

The gap is not subtle. It widens with scale, because the dominant
line items on the self-hosted side (egress, then ops) scale roughly
linearly with volume, whereas CoreLink's bill is dominated by a flat
tier fee with small per-GB overage that does not include egress.

## What this comparison does not include

A few honest caveats so the comparison is not just the optimistic
side of the ledger:

- **CI minutes.** None of the columns include CI compute minutes. The
  customer is paying their CI vendor for those separately, and the
  cost is the same across all four columns. We hold it constant for
  clarity.
- **Inner-loop developer-hour savings.** CoreLink's primary value
  prop, beyond pure unit economics, is faster cache hits → faster
  builds → developer-hour savings. None of the columns above include
  that benefit, because it is a customer-side benefit that does not
  appear on the CoreLink invoice. The
  [economics post](/blog/05-fast-cache-hit-economics/) walks the
  arithmetic; the [calculator](./calculator) computes it for the
  customer's inputs.
- **Reserved-capacity discounts.** Heavy S3 customers can negotiate
  reserved capacity and committed-use discounts that compress the
  self-hosted columns by 10–30%. We use list prices. If a customer
  has aggressive cloud-spend commits, the gap narrows but does not
  close.
- **Bring-your-own-bucket on CoreLink.** Enterprise customers can
  bring their own R2 bucket; this shifts storage onto the customer's
  Cloudflare bill and removes the CoreLink storage line item entirely.
  We do not model that here because it is an Enterprise option, not a
  self-serve one.
- **The operational tax estimate is a model, not a measurement.**
  Real teams report 0.5–2× of the hours we listed. Customers running
  mature SRE practices will be on the low end; customers running
  understaffed dev-tools teams will be on the high end. The model is
  defensible at the mid-point and the rank-order of the comparison
  does not change under either bound.
- **Harbor is a registry, not a remote cache.** The comparison is
  apples-to-oranges by construction and is included only because
  customers do ask. The honest answer is: do not run Bazel against
  Harbor. Run Bazel against a REAPI-conformant cache.
