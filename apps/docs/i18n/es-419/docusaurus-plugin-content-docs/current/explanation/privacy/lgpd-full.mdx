# Your LGPD rights at CoreLink (full menu)

> **DRAFT — pending Legal + DPO review.** Technical claims below are accurate as of 2026-05-15 and pinned to commit `fb9f56e`. The companion compliance docs (`LGPD-FULL-AUDIT-2026-05-15.md`, `LGPD-ROPA-2026-05-15.md`) are the source of truth; this page is the customer-facing summary.

CoreLink is built in Brazil and our anchor customer (Forge) is Brazilian. We
take LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018) seriously, not as a marketing checkbox. The
sister page [Brazil residency & LGPD Art. 33 §1º](/residency/lgpd-brazil)
covers *where* your data lives. **This page covers everything else**: what
data we hold, what rights you have, how to use them, and what we will do for
you (and how fast).

## 1. The short version

If you are a CoreLink user or your data is processed by CoreLink because a
tenant uploaded a blob containing it, **LGPD gives you nine concrete rights**.
We have built all nine into the platform as self-service APIs — no support
ticket required, no waiting for a human to click a button (with one
exception: revoking marketing consent is instantaneous, but deleting your
whole account requires a confirmation step-up to make sure it's really you).

The nine rights, our service level for each, and how to exercise them:

| LGPD Art. 18 right | What it means | Our SLA | How to exercise |
|---|---|---|---|
| §I — Confirmação | Yes/no answer: are you processing my data? | 5 business days | `POST /v1/privacy/dsr/access` (subset query) or via the admin UI |
| §II — Acesso | Give me a copy of everything you have on me | 15 business days | `POST /v1/privacy/dsr/access` |
| §III — Correção | Fix incorrect, incomplete, or outdated data | 5 business days | `POST /v1/privacy/dsr/rectification` (requires MFA) |
| §IV — Anonimização / bloqueio / eliminação | Delete unnecessary, excessive, or unlawfully-processed data | 30 calendar days | `POST /v1/privacy/dsr/erasure` (requires MFA) |
| §V — Portabilidade | Give me my data in a structured format I can take elsewhere | 15 business days | `POST /v1/privacy/dsr/portability` |
| §VI — Eliminação (consent-based) | Delete data we hold under your consent | 30 calendar days | Same erasure endpoint as §IV |
| §VII — Compartilhamento | Tell me everyone you shared my data with | 15 business days | `GET /v1/privacy/dsr/shared-with` |
| §VIII — Consequências de não consentir | Tell me what happens if I say no | Continuous (shown at consent capture) | Read the privacy notice §5 or look at the consent form before clicking |
| §IX — Revogação do consentimento | Unsubscribe / withdraw consent | **Immediate (≤ 5 minutes)** | `DELETE /v1/consent/<purpose>` |

Plus a bonus tenth right per the 2019 amendment:

| Right | What it means | Our SLA | How to exercise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Art. 18 §II amendment — Oposição | I want you to stop processing under legitimate interest | 15 business days | `POST /v1/privacy/dsr/objection` |

If you prefer talking to a human, email `privacy@hugr.com` and our DPO will
process your request through the same pipeline (with the same SLA — we time
it from the moment we verify your identity, not when you sent the email).

## 2. What data do we actually hold?

A short, honest answer.

**About you as a CoreLink user:** your email address, your name, your billing
address, your hashed password or WebAuthn public key, the IP address and
user-agent class of your last few requests (truncated and pseudonymized),
your DSR requests, your consent grants and revocations, and your support
tickets.

**About your data (blobs you upload):** we hold the bytes you upload, a
content-addressable hash of those bytes (the digest), some metadata
(size, timestamp, your tenant ID as a random UUID), and an audit record
that says "this tenant uploaded this digest at this time" — **but we do
not look at the content of your blobs**. Under LGPD, when your blob
contains personal data of someone else (e.g., you uploaded a dataset for
ML training that contains your customers' info), **you are the controller
and we are the operator**. We do not read or use your data outside the
contract.

**What we don't hold:** we do not hold payment card data (Stripe does that;
we never see it). We do not hold biometric templates (your WebAuthn key is
a public cryptographic identifier, not a biometric — the actual biometric
never leaves your device). We do not knowingly hold data on people under
18, because CoreLink is a B2B platform and our Terms of Service require
account holders to be adults representing a business entity.

**The complete inventory:** 14 distinct dataflows are catalogued in our
[Record of Processing Activities](/privacy/sub-processors) (Art. 37 +
Art. 41). Each one specifies the data category, purpose, legal basis,
retention period, recipients, and security measures.

## 3. Why are we allowed to hold each piece?

LGPD Art. 7 lists ten lawful bases for processing personal data. We use
four of them, **fixed per purpose** — meaning we cannot quietly swap your
data from "consent" to "legitimate interest" without forcing you to
re-consent through a new version of our privacy notice. That immutability
is enforced in code (`crates/corelink-privacy-consent-ledger/`) and tested
with 10,000 property-test cases per CI run.

| Why we hold the data | LGPD base | Example |
|---|---|---|
| To run the platform you signed up for | Art. 7 §V — contract | CAS uploads, AC entries, your account |
| Because the law requires it | Art. 7 §II — legal obligation | Audit logs (SOC 2 + Art. 37), fiscal records (Art. 16 §3, 5 years), DSR fulfillment |
| To prevent abuse and fraud | Art. 7 §IX — legitimate interest | Security monitoring, anomaly detection |
| Because you said yes | Art. 7 §I — consent | Marketing emails, personalized analytics, opt-in ML training, beta features |

If your data is held under consent, you can revoke at any time and we will
stop processing within 5 minutes. **We will not silently fall back to
"legitimate interest"** when your consent expires — processing pauses until
you re-consent or delete the data. This is intentional and documented in
our privacy model.

## 4. How fast will we respond?

The clock starts when we verify your identity (we ask for a step-up MFA
re-auth on destructive requests — erasure and rectification — to make sure
nobody hijacks your account to ask us to delete your data). The clock
stops when we mark your request `completed`.

We can pause the clock only in three narrow cases:

- You are under a **legal hold** (court order, regulatory subpoena, ongoing
  investigation). We will tell you the request is paused and why.
- Your request is **ambiguous** and we asked you for clarification. The
  clock resumes when you answer.
- **Technical impossibility** documented in our incident records. Cap of 5
  business days total per request.

You will receive an email confirmation at every state transition
(`verified`, `in_progress`, `completed`, or `rejected`). Receipts are
cryptographically signed (RS256 JWT, KMS-rooted, 90-day anti-replay) so you
can prove later that the request happened.

## 5. The deletion ("eliminação") in detail

When you ask us to delete your data under Art. 18 §IV, the deletion
pipeline propagates the request across **12 backends** in a canonical
order. Eight backends are **effectively erased** (rows hard-deleted); four
are **pseudonymized retention** (the data stays because we are legally
required to keep it — audit logs, billing records, point-in-time backups —
but your identity is replaced with a one-way HKDF hash so we cannot
re-identify you).

**The 8 effective-erasure targets:**

1. **Neon** (`dsr_tickets`, `account`, `tenant`, `user_account`,
   `consent_ledger`, `subscription`) — hard delete of your rows, with the
   DSR ticket itself preserved as proof of the deletion.
2. **Neon billing detail** (`invoice`, `usage_event`) — retained under
   legal hold per LGPD Art. 16 §3 (5-year fiscal/accounting retention).
3. **R2 CAS** (content-addressable storage) — refcount-based eraser. If
   your blob is shared with other subjects or referenced by other tenants,
   we remove your reference but keep the blob until everyone has dropped
   it (plus 72-hour grace). If your blob is only referenced by you, we
   tombstone immediately and the garbage collector reclaims it.
4. **R2 AC** (Action Cache) — every cached entry referencing your subject
   ID is invalidated; downstream caches are notified.
5. **D1 metadata** — subject-scoped rows deleted; refcount synced with R2.
6. **KV** — session tokens and cached subject metadata invalidated.
7. **Stripe** — your `Customer` record is updated with email/name/address
   nullified (Stripe does not allow full deletion because it would break
   invoice integrity under PCI rules — but we strip every piece of PII
   they have on you).
8. **Loki / Grafana** — log deletion API applied with retention compaction
   triggered.

**The 4 pseudonymized-retention targets:**

9. **R2 audit log** (Object Lock 7y) — we cannot delete audit records, so
   your subject ID is replaced with `erased_<HKDF-hash>` (one-way; the
   info string is `corelink/v1/audit-pseudonym`).
10. **Backup PITR** (Neon point-in-time recovery, 30-day retention) —
    natural rotation expires it within 30 days; if anyone restores from a
    backup during that window, a tombstone replay re-applies your erasure.
11. **R2 CAS legal-hold partition** (governance mode) — content retained if
    actively under hold; index references are pseudonymized.
12. **Compliance evidence store** (LIA records, consent proof, DSR
    evidence — 7y retention per framework) — your subject ID is
    pseudonymized.

You will receive a final `dsr.completed.v1` event with a hash of the entire
deletion chain, and an email confirming we are done. Total SLA: 30
calendar days.

## 6. Who else sees your data?

LGPD Art. 18 §VII gives you the right to know everyone we shared your data
with. The full list is at
[/privacy/sub-processors](/privacy/sub-processors). The short version, for
a default Brazilian tenant configuration (no BYOK, no opt-in analytics):

- **Cloudflare** — runs our infrastructure (Workers, R2, D1, KV, DO). Data
  stays in São Paulo for `sam`-region tenants (see the
  [residency page](/residency/lgpd-brazil)).
- **Neon EU** — runs our control-plane Postgres. **This is an
  international transfer**: Neon does not currently offer a Brazilian
  region, so billing detail for Brazilian tenants is in Frankfurt. We rely
  on LGPD Art. 33 §V (contractual necessity for billing) with SCC Modules
  2/3 in the Neon DPA.
- **Stripe** — processes payments. Another international transfer (US)
  under Art. 33 §V + Stripe's DPA with Brazil addendum + PCI-DSS Level 1.
  We send Stripe a pseudonymized customer ID, your email, and the amount.
  No card data ever touches CoreLink.
- **Clerk** — handles authentication tokens. US-hosted; Art. 33 §V; only
  auth tokens + email cross the border.
- **AWS São Paulo / GCP southamerica-east1 / Azure Brazil South** — host
  your BYOK envelope key if you opt in to BYOK. Brazilian regions only for
  Brazilian tenants. We never see the underlying key material.

Four other sub-processors (Sentry, PostHog, LogRocket, Stripe Atlas
counsel) are **disabled by default for Brazilian tenants** until we
complete our enhanced review (tracked as GAP-14, ETA D+60). If you want
them enabled, you would opt in with explicit consent under Art. 33 §I.

If a new sub-processor would touch your data, we notify you 30 days in
advance per CTRL-PRIV-021 (privacy_model.md §5.3).

## 7. What about consent?

You give consent at signup for `service_delivery` and `account_management`
(both `Art. 7 §V` contract base — not consent, and not revocable without
terminating the contract). You can give or withhold consent independently
for each of the following:

| Purpose | What we do with it | Default | Revoke time |
|---|---|---|---|
| `analytics_personalized` | Per-tenant dashboards with reidentifiable PII | OFF | ≤ 5 min |
| `marketing_email` | Newsletter, product updates | OFF | ≤ 5 min |
| `marketing_research` | Surveys, NPS | OFF | ≤ 5 min |
| `beta_features` | Preview features with enriched telemetry | OFF | ≤ 5 min |
| `third_party_integrations` | Stripe, GitHub, custom webhooks | OFF (per-integration) | ≤ 5 min |
| `cross_tenant_benchmarks` | Aggregated leaderboards | OFF | ≤ 5 min |
| `training_ml_models` | ML cache prediction with k-anonymity | OFF | ≤ 5 min |

Each consent record we store carries: the **exact text** you saw (hashed
with SHA-256), the **version** of the privacy notice (so we can re-check
what you agreed to even years later), the **locale** of the form (Portuguese
/ English / Spanish), the **wording ID** of the specific checkbox label, and
two timestamps (when the UI rendered the notice and when you clicked). This
is what LGPD Art. 8 calls "manifestação livre, informada e inequívoca" —
demonstrably "informed" requires being able to prove what you actually saw.

## 8. Special cases

**You are a subject whose data was uploaded by a tenant.** You can file a
DSR with us, and we will route the request to the tenant who controls that
data (we are the operator under DPA Art. 7). The tenant has the same 30-day
SLA. If the tenant does not respond, you can escalate to ANPD with a copy
of our routing record.

**You operate under legal hold.** We will tell you which of your data is on
hold and why. The clock pauses on the affected scope but continues on
everything else.

**You request data that triggers another subject's privacy.** We will
redact references to other subjects in your export bundle. This is
mandatory under LGPD Art. 9 + Art. 10 (data minimization in disclosure).

**Your child is a subject.** Out of scope for CoreLink. Per ToS, account
holders must be ≥ 18 representing a business. If a tenant uploaded data
about minors, the tenant is the controller (Art. 14 obligations are on
the tenant, not on HuGR).

## 9. Incidents and breach notification (Art. 46-48)

If we suffer a personal data breach that creates relevant risk or damage
to you, LGPD Art. 48 obliges us to notify ANPD and notify you. Our
internal timeline (conservative versus ANPD Resolução 15/2024
requirements):

- Detection to internal SEV-1 declaration: ≤ 1 hour.
- Declaration to triage and containment: ≤ 4 hours.
- Triage to ANPD notification: ≤ 48 hours (ANPD allows 3 business days; we
  commit 48h).
- Notification to affected data subjects: ≤ 72 hours after ANPD.
- Public post-mortem (if material): ≤ 14 days.

We tabletop-exercise this twice a year (`specs/_compliance/IR-TABLETOP-PLAYBOOK.md`).
You will receive the breach notification by email at the address on your
account, plus an in-app banner if you have an active session.
