# BYOK — customer-managed keys

> **DRAFT — pending Legal + Finance + Seguridad review.** Provider availability
> below tracks the engineering rollout in `compliance/byok-fips-matrix.md` and
> may change before GA.

CoreLink Enterprise tenants can supply their own root key in a KMS they own.
CoreLink uses envelope encryption: a per-tenant data encryption key (DEK) is
generated locally, used to encrypt blobs, and then wrapped by the customer's
KMS root key. Without the customer's KMS, ciphertext is unrecoverable.

## Supported KMS providers

| Provider | FIPS posture | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AWS KMS | FIPS 140-3 Level 1 (CMVP #4523) | Available |
| GCP Cloud KMS | FIPS 140-2 Level 1 | Rollout in progress (WI-S14-005) |
| Azure Key Vault Premium (HSM) | FIPS 140-2 Level 2 | Rollout in progress (WI-S14-005) |
| HashiCorp Vault Enterprise (FIPS build) | FIPS 140-3 Level 1 | Rollout in progress (WI-S14-005) |

Provider-by-provider validation evidence lives in `compliance/byok-fips-matrix.md`
and is reviewed quarterly.

## Operational guarantees

- **Key revocation** — if the customer revokes our IAM grant on the root key,
  CoreLink cannot decrypt new blob fetches within one DEK cache TTL (≤ 60s).
- **Rotation** — Tenant Derivation Keys rotate annually; the customer can
  rotate the underlying root key on any schedule and CoreLink re-wraps DEKs
  during the next maintenance window.
- **Break-glass** — `RB-KEY-COMPROMISE` runbook applies; on suspected
  compromise CoreLink rotates DEKs and invalidates tokens emitted under the
  compromised key.

## Limitations

- BYOK is available on the **Enterprise** tier (see [pricing](../../pricing)).
- BYOK does not change the SOC 2 or ISO 27001 scope of CoreLink itself.
- BYOK does not exempt the customer from RFC 6962 audit-log retention.
