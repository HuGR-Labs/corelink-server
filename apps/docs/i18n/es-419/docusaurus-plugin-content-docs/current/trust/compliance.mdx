# Compliance

This page answers the four questions every procurement team asks:

1. *What's your SOC 2 status?*
2. *Are you LGPD / GDPR compliant?*
3. *Can we use you for HIPAA or PCI workloads?*
4. *Where's the paperwork?*

The honest answer to each is below. If you need the underlying evidence
(SOC 2 readiness rollup, DPA, gap register, residency attestation), email
`trust@humangr.com` with your countersigned NDA — we route packs back within
1 business day.

## SOC 2

### Status

- **Target framework:** AICPA TSC 2017 with 2022 points-of-focus updates.
- **Trust principles in scope:** Security (CC1..CC9), Availability (A1),
  Confidentiality (C1), Processing Integrity (PI1), Privacy
  (P-DSR / P-CONSENT / P-BREACH).
- **Audit partner:** Schellman & Co., LLC (engagement letter executed R5-1).
- **Continuous-evidence platform:** Drata.
- **Type I (point-in-time) audit:** target fieldwork **Q4 2026**, report
  publication early Q1 2027.
- **Type II (operating-effectiveness) audit:** target fieldwork **Q3 2027**
  after a minimum 6-month observation window, report Q4 2027.
- **Current readiness:** **83.7%** weighted (113 of 135 weighted
  criterion-points green) as of the 2026-05-15 pre-staging snapshot.
- **Type I projected pass-rate:** 95% combined (75% unqualified + 20%
  qualified narrow scope) per internal 2026-05-14 readiness model.

### What 83.7% actually means

It is **not** a marketing percentage. The number comes from a per-criterion
R/Y/G scorecard:

| State | Count (43 in-scope) | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Implemented (green) | 27 | 62.8% |
| Partial (yellow — remediation in flight) | 14 | 32.6% |
| Gap (red — blocking-GA) | 2 | 4.6% |

The 2 reds today are both linked to **BYOK FIPS attestation per provider
(GAP-02 — CC6.1 + C1.1)** and have a hard D+30 closure cap with a fallback
ADR if attestation letters slip. All major gaps close before Type I
fieldwork; all minor gaps close before the Type II observation window cuts.

If you want the full 33-gap register with owner / effort / target dates,
email `trust@humangr.com`.

### What you can rely on today (pre-Type-I)

These controls are implemented and continuously monitored, even though the
external attestation is still in flight:

- **CC6.1 / CC6.7 — logical access + cryptographic protection:** TLS 1.3
  enforced edge-to-origin, MFA-enforced admin plane, signed-deploy
  pipeline, Rekor transparency-log entries for every release.
- **CC7.1 — vulnerability management:** Dependency-Track + cargo-deny +
  cargo-fuzz with daily evidence collection. See
  [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/HumanGuardrail/corelink/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
- **CC7.2 — system monitoring:** SLO catalogue + Prometheus metrics +
  PagerDuty 24/7 rotation across 3 regions.
- **CC8.1 — change management:** branch protection + dual-approval pattern
  (`PAT-DUAL-APPROVAL-001`) + Cosign-verified releases.
- **PI1.2 — auditability:** append-only, Merkle-linked audit chain with
  RFC 6962 inclusion proofs. See [Audit chain](/security/audit-chain).

### CSA STAR / ISO 27001

We crosswalk to **ISO 27001:2022 Annex A** in detail on the dedicated
[ISO 27001 page](./iso27001) — **98.9% in-scope coverage today** (89 of 90
applicable Annex A controls Implemented or in-flight) with **91% SOC 2
overlap**. Formal **ISO 27001 certification target: Q1-2027** (Stage 1 audit
Q4-2026 stacked with SOC 2 Type I fieldwork; Stage 2 audit + certificate
issuance Q1-2027). Audit partner: Schellman. We also crosswalk to **NIST
800-53 Rev 5 Moderate** (87% covered as of 2026-05-15; GAP-24 closes T+6m).

## LGPD (Brazil — Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados)

- **Status:** **Compliant.** CoreLink processes Brazilian-tenant data
  in-region (`sam`) with full Art. 33 §1º residency attestation.
- **DPO:** in-place. DPO contact: `dpo@humangr.com`.
- **Residency attestation:** see
  [Brazil residency & LGPD](/residency/lgpd-brazil) for the customer-facing
  explainer. The internal attestation document is
  `specs/_compliance/LGPD-RESIDENCY-ATTESTATION-2026-05-15.md`; DPO + Security
  Lead countersign closing 2026-05-16. The attestation is verified nightly
  by `scripts/verify-lgpd-residency.py`.
- **DSR turnaround (Art. 18):** acknowledgement within 5 business days;
  resolution within 15 business days unless extended per Art. 19 §1.
- **Cross-border transfers:** SCCs incorporated into the DPA; supplementary
  measures documented per the EDPB recommendation.
- **Breach notification:** within **72 hours** to the ANPD per Art. 33,
  using our pre-registered communication channel.

## GDPR (EU)

- **Status:** **Compliant** as a processor (and joint controller for limited
  service-telemetry purposes).
- **DPA:** signed template available at `legal/dpa/v1.0.0`. We countersign
  customer-paper DPAs only when the variance is immaterial; otherwise we
  route to our template plus an addendum.
- **Cross-border transfers (Schrems II):** SCC Modules 2/3 in the DPA plus
  supplementary measures (BYOK envelope encryption, EU-region pin).
- **Breach notification:** within **72 hours** of awareness to the supervisory
  authority per Art. 33, and to affected data subjects per Art. 34 when the
  risk is high.
- **Data subject rights (Arts. 15-22):** access, rectification, erasure,
  portability, restriction, objection. Tooling documented in
  [Data handling → Retention](./data-handling#retention).
- **Sub-processor change notice:** **30 calendar days** in advance via the
  channel registered on the [sub-processors page](./subprocessors).

## HIPAA (U.S. healthcare)

- **Status:** **Out of scope by design.**
- **Rationale:** CoreLink is a build-artefact cache. It does not handle
  Protected Health Information (PHI). We will not sign Business Associate
  Agreements (BAAs) for production use of the platform in PHI workflows.
- **What this means in practice:** the underlying infrastructure providers
  (Cloudflare, AWS, GCP, Azure) are themselves HIPAA-aligned, so the
  raw substrate could in principle support PHI. CoreLink's product surface
  is not engineered, scoped, or tested for PHI, and we do not commit to the
  Privacy / Security / Breach Notification rules.
- **If your build artefacts contain PHI:** that's likely an upstream tagging
  bug; please raise a SEV-2 with your account team.

## PCI DSS

- **Status:** **SAQ-A compliant (self-attested 2026-05-15)** — CoreLink itself is *not* in the PCI cardholder data environment (CDE). See the dedicated [PCI DSS posture page](./pci-dss) for the full breakdown.
- **How payments are handled:** All cardholder data is tokenized at the
  edge via Stripe Elements; CoreLink never sees a PAN. Our billing
  integration follows the **SAQ-A** path (merchant outsourcing all card
  handling to a validated third-party provider).
- **Stripe attestation:** Stripe, Inc. is PCI-DSS Level 1 (latest AOC on file
  via Drata vendor module).
- **What's stored on our side:** Stripe customer IDs and subscription IDs
  only. Last-four digits and brand are surfaced through Stripe's APIs at
  read time; never persisted on our control plane.
- **Evidence on file (request via `trust@humangr.com`):** signed SAQ-A questionnaire (`specs/_compliance/PCI-DSS-SAQ-A-2026-05-15.md`), boundary + data-flow diagram (`specs/_compliance/PCI-DSS-BOUNDARY-DIAGRAM.md`), annual recertification checklist (`specs/_compliance/PCI-DSS-ANNUAL-RECERTIFY.md`).
- **Next recertification:** 2027-05-15 (annual cadence).
