## How billing works

- **Storage** is measured in GB-hours and aggregated into GB-months on the
  monthly invoice cycle.
- **Egress** is measured per origin region; intra-CoreLink replication is not
  billed.
- **Cache hits** do not consume egress; only cross-region pulls do.

Use the [pricing calculator](./calculator) to estimate cost based on your
cache-hit ratio and storage volume.
