and we have a calendared path to a certificate by Q1-2027"**. The detail
below explains what that means in practice.

## Why 80%+ of the work overlaps with SOC 2

ISO 27001:2022 Annex A and SOC 2 TSC 2017 (with 2022 PoF) share most of their
control substance. They differ mostly in:

- **Documentation formality** (ISO expects a formal asset register; SOC 2
  accepts a Drata-driven inventory).
- **Process scaffolding** (ISO expects clauses 4–10 documenting the ISMS as a
  process; SOC 2 looks at the controls themselves).
- **Annual cadence** (ISO has surveillance audits in year 1 + 2 and full
  recertification in year 3; SOC 2 has Type I → Type II → annual Type II).

Because we already produce auditor-grade evidence for 91% of the criteria
that crosswalk to ISO Annex A — collected automatically by Drata — the
incremental work to reach an ISO 27001 certificate is small.

## Roadmap to Q1-2027 certification

The certification path stacks on top of our SOC 2 Type I fieldwork. Same
audit firm, same evidence collection, separate engagement letter.

| Milestone | Window | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M0 — ISMS scope statement | T+0 .. T+1m | Scope doc + SoA refreshed to 93-row 2022 format |
| M1 — Clauses 4–10 documentation | T+1m .. T+3m | ISO 27001 process clauses written (mostly consolidation of existing material) |
| M2 — Internal audit (first cycle) | T+3m .. T+4m | 5-day engagement with 3rd-party ISO 27001 Lead Auditor |
| M3 — Management review (first cycle) | T+4m | Owner-led 4h review per §9.3 |
| **M4 — Stage 1 audit (documentation review)** | **Q4-2026** | Schellman; stacked with SOC 2 Type I fieldwork |
| M5 — Corrective actions from Stage 1 | T+6m..T+7m | Close any Stage 1 non-conformities |
| **M6 — Stage 2 audit (operational effectiveness)** | **Q1-2027** | Schellman; sample testing across Annex A |
| **M7 — Certificate issuance** | **Q1-2027** | ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certificate posted; 3-year cycle begins |

After certificate issuance:

- **Surveillance audit year 1:** Q1-2028.
- **Surveillance audit year 2:** Q1-2029.
- **Recertification (full re-audit):** Q1-2030.

## What this means for procurement / RFP teams

- **If you need an ISO 27001 certificate before Q1-2027:** we can give you a
  signed crosswalk + gap analysis + roadmap (this page + the two internal
  companion docs under NDA). Most enterprise procurement teams accept this
  as sufficient for vendor-onboarding when the SOC 2 Type I report is
  imminent.
- **If you need the certificate itself:** the date is **Q1-2027**. We will
  not commit to an earlier date because Stage 1 → Stage 2 → certificate
  issuance has irreducible accredited-body lead time.
- **If you need ISO 27701 (PIMS):** that's a separate certification
  extension. We currently target **2028** for ISO 27701 (post-Type-II
  + post-27001-Stage-2). The substance (privacy by design, DPIA, DSR,
  consent, breach notification) is already covered under LGPD + GDPR + SOC 2
  Privacy.
