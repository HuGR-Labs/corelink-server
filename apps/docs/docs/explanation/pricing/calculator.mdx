# Cost calculator

**Understanding-oriented.** This page shows the exact arithmetic
CoreLink uses to compute a customer's monthly bill, and walks three
worked examples at small / medium / large scale. For tier definitions,
see [pricing overview](./). For comparisons against self-hosted
alternatives, see [vs. alternatives](./comparison).

> **DRAFT — pending Finance + Marketing + Product sign-off.** The
> arithmetic is canonical; the constants are launch-day targets and
> may shift inside the placeholder window until Finance signs the
> audit trail. Customers in a procurement cycle should ask Sales for
> the contractual rate card.

## Inputs

The calculator takes six inputs. All of them are observable from any
existing cache (you do not need to run CoreLink first to estimate the
bill).

| Symbol | Input | Units | Typical range |
|---|---|---|---|
| `T` | Tenants | count | 1–50 |
| `S_w` | Monthly CAS write volume | GB / mo | 5–5000 |
| `S_r` | Monthly CAS read volume | TB / mo | 0.05–50 |
| `byok` | BYOK provider | `none` / `aws` / `gcp` / `azure` | — |
| `h` | Cache hit rate | fraction `[0, 1]` | 0.5–0.95 |
| `R` | Region pin | count of regions | 1–4 |

- `T` is the number of distinct tenants under one billing account.
  Solo developers and most small teams have `T = 1`. Multi-product
  orgs that want billing isolation per product have `T > 1`.
- `S_w` is monthly **net** CAS storage growth after dedup and eviction.
  Bazel monorepos at steady state are typically 10–200 GB; large
  Buck2 monorepos run 500 GB to a few TB.
- `S_r` is monthly **read** volume (TB), which scales with CI run
  volume × per-run cache lookup × per-lookup blob size. Hit rate
  matters here because misses do not generate reads.
- `byok` toggles the BYOK add-on on Team. Free / Starter do not
  support BYOK; Enterprise includes BYOK at no extra charge.
- `h` is the hit rate. The calculator does **not** charge for cache
  misses (a miss is a `404` and the customer's runner builds locally),
  but `h` is included so the calculator can also report inner-loop
  savings (the "cache tax" model from the
  [economics blog post](/blog/05-fast-cache-hit-economics/)).
- `R` is the count of distinct R2 regions the tenant pins to. Free /
  Starter pin to 1; Team pins to up to 2; Enterprise pins to all 4.
  Extra regions on Team and Enterprise replicate writes (a `2×`
  multiplier on `S_w` for billable storage).

## Formula

The exact monthly invoice is:

```
# 1. Resolve effective storage and reads after replication and dedup.
S_w_billable = S_w × replication_factor(R, tier)
S_r_billable = S_r × 1024                  # convert TB → GB for billing

# 2. Resolve included quotas by tier (see table below).
S_w_included = INCLUDED_STORAGE_GB[tier] × T
S_r_included = INCLUDED_READS_GB[tier]   × T

# 3. Compute overage on storage and reads.
overage_storage = max(0, S_w_billable - S_w_included) × STORAGE_RATE[tier]
overage_reads   = max(0, S_r_billable - S_r_included) × READ_RATE[tier]

# 4. Resolve add-ons (BYOK on Team only; included on Enterprise).
addon_byok = (BYOK_FEE[tier] if byok != "none" else 0)

# 5. Resolve base fee. Free is $0; Starter / Team are flat;
#    Enterprise is negotiated (calculator returns a quote-request CTA).
invoice = BASE_FEE[tier] × T + overage_storage + overage_reads + addon_byok
```

`replication_factor(R, tier)` is:

| `R` regions | Free | Starter | Team | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| 2 | n/a | n/a | 2.0 | 2.0 |
| 3 | n/a | n/a | n/a | 3.0 |
| 4 | n/a | n/a | n/a | 4.0 |

Reads are not replicated for billing (a read serves one runner, not
all regions).

## Constants

These are the rate-card constants the formula references.

| Tier | `BASE_FEE` ($/mo) | `INCLUDED_STORAGE_GB` | `INCLUDED_READS_GB` | `STORAGE_RATE` ($/GB/mo) | `READ_RATE` ($/GB) | `BYOK_FEE` ($/mo) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 0 | 5 | 50 | hard-capped | hard-capped | n/a |
| Starter | 29 | 50 | 500 | 0.20 | 0.04 | n/a |
| Team | 199 | 500 | 5,120 | 0.15 | 0.03 | 99 |
| Enterprise | quote | quote | quote | quote | quote | included |

"hard-capped" means Free returns `429` past the included quota until
the next calendar month resets. Free does not generate an invoice.

## Tier indicator

The calculator picks the indicated tier as the cheapest tier that
covers the customer's inputs without overage, falling back to overage
on the next-larger tier if needed:

```
indicated_tier =
  Free        if S_w ≤ 5  AND S_r × 1024 ≤ 50  AND T = 1 AND byok = "none" AND R ≤ 1
  Starter     if S_w ≤ 50 AND S_r × 1024 ≤ 500 AND T ≤ 5 AND byok = "none" AND R ≤ 1
  Team        if S_w ≤ 500 AND S_r ≤ 5         AND T ≤ 10                  AND R ≤ 2
  Enterprise  otherwise
```

If the customer overshoots a tier on one dimension but is otherwise
fine, the calculator picks that tier and reports the overage; if they
overshoot on three or more dimensions or trigger BYOK / 3+ regions /
`T > 10`, the calculator routes to the Enterprise inquiry form.

## Per-tier price table (worked)

For a representative input — `T = 1`, `S_w = 100 GB/mo`, `S_r = 2 TB/mo`,
`byok = none`, `h = 0.8`, `R = 1` — the per-tier price is:

| Tier | Base | Storage overage | Read overage | BYOK | Total | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | hard-cap (over) | hard-cap (over) | n/a | n/a | exceeds Free cap |
| Starter | $29 | (100−50) × $0.20 = $10 | (2048−500) × $0.04 = $61.92 | n/a | **$100.92** | indicated tier candidate |
| Team | $199 | $0 (100 ≤ 500) | $0 (2048 ≤ 5120) | $0 | **$199.00** | no overage |
| Enterprise | quote | quote | quote | included | **quote** | overkill at this scale |

The calculator surfaces all four lines so the customer sees the
break-even tier (Team is cheaper than Starter-with-overage above ~120 GB
storage or ~3 TB reads, depending on the mix).

## Three worked scale points

### Small: solo dev on Bazel

`T = 1`, `S_w = 4 GB/mo`, `S_r = 40 GB/mo`, `byok = none`, `h = 0.75`, `R = 1`.

| Tier | Total | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | **$0** | fits within Free cap |
| Starter | $29 | over-served |
| Team | $199 | over-served |
| Enterprise | quote | wildly over-served |

Indicated tier: **Free**.

### Medium: 200-engineer Bazel monorepo, single region

`T = 1`, `S_w = 180 GB/mo`, `S_r = 4 TB/mo`, `byok = none`, `h = 0.82`, `R = 1`.

| Tier | Total | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | n/a | exceeds cap |
| Starter | $29 + (130 × $0.20) + ((4096 − 500) × $0.04) = $29 + $26 + $143.84 = **$198.84** | overage on both axes |
| Team | $199 + $0 + $0 = **$199.00** | included quotas cover it |
| Enterprise | quote | overkill |

Indicated tier: **Team** (effectively tied with Starter at this size,
which is by design — Team is priced as the natural graduation
threshold).

### Large: multi-region enterprise, BYOK, 5 tenants

`T = 5`, `S_w = 800 GB/mo`, `S_r = 12 TB/mo`, `byok = aws`, `h = 0.88`, `R = 3`.

| Tier | Total | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | n/a | exceeds cap |
| Starter | n/a | exceeds tenant + region cap |
| Team | n/a | exceeds region cap (`R = 3 > 2`) |
| Enterprise | **quote** | required (3 regions, BYOK, multi-tenant) |

Indicated tier: **Enterprise**. The calculator routes to the inquiry
form with a pre-filled quote-request payload containing the inputs
above. Per WI-S19-005, Enterprise customers cannot self-serve through
Stripe Checkout; they must complete the inquiry form and receive a
contractual rate from Sales.

## Inner-loop savings (informational)

For each set of inputs, the calculator also reports the annualized
inner-loop cache-tax savings using the model from
[the economics post](/blog/05-fast-cache-hit-economics/):

```
annual_cache_tax_saved
  ≈ engineers
  × builds_per_engineer_per_day
  × jobs_per_build
  × cache_lookups_per_job
  × (h × (baseline_hit_latency_s - corelink_hit_latency_s))
  × loaded_engineer_hourly_rate
  × working_days_per_year
  / 3600
```

This is *not* part of the bill (it is the customer's avoided cost on
their existing cache, not a CoreLink line item) but is the
break-even-relevant number when comparing CoreLink to a self-hosted
baseline. The [comparison page](./comparison) walks this arithmetic
explicitly at three scale points.

## What the calculator deliberately does not do

- It does not assume a specific hit-rate improvement from migrating
  to CoreLink. We do not promise a specific hit-rate delta because it
  depends on the customer's build graph. The customer enters their
  current observed hit rate; CoreLink's hit-rate budget is
  policy-pinned but the absolute number is the customer's.
- It does not assume a specific developer-hour-saved-per-build
  number. The economics model exposes that input; the customer enters
  their loaded-cost-per-hour. We don't pick it for them.
- It does not give Enterprise an estimated price. Enterprise is
  always quote-routed because the dimensions that push a customer to
  Enterprise (BYOK, multi-region, multi-tenant, custom DPA) are not
  uniform across customers and the rate card varies. The calculator
  emits a pre-filled quote-request, not a number.

## Copy-pasteable formula (for spreadsheets)

This is the same arithmetic in spreadsheet-friendly form. Paste each
line into a cell of your favorite spreadsheet (Excel, Google Sheets,
Numbers, LibreOffice) and replace the named-range references with
cell coordinates.

```
# Inputs
T                = 1
S_w_gb           = 180
S_r_tb           = 4
byok             = "none"      # "none" | "aws" | "gcp" | "azure"
h                = 0.82
R                = 1

# Tier rate-card (Starter / Team — Free is $0, Enterprise is quote)
base_starter     = 29
incl_storage_starter = 50
incl_reads_starter   = 500
rate_storage_starter = 0.20
rate_reads_starter   = 0.04

base_team        = 199
incl_storage_team    = 500
incl_reads_team      = 5120
rate_storage_team    = 0.15
rate_reads_team      = 0.03
byok_fee_team        = 99

# Derived
S_w_billable     = S_w_gb * IF(R<=1, 1, IF(R=2, 2, IF(R=3, 3, 4)))
S_r_billable_gb  = S_r_tb * 1024

# Per-tier totals
total_starter    = base_starter * T
                 + MAX(0, S_w_billable - incl_storage_starter * T) * rate_storage_starter
                 + MAX(0, S_r_billable_gb - incl_reads_starter * T) * rate_reads_starter

total_team       = base_team * T
                 + MAX(0, S_w_billable - incl_storage_team * T) * rate_storage_team
                 + MAX(0, S_r_billable_gb - incl_reads_team * T) * rate_reads_team
                 + IF(byok = "none", 0, byok_fee_team)
```

The internal version of this calculator with margin analysis (vendor
cost / sale price / gross margin per tier) lives at
`marketing/sales/PRICING-WORKSHEET.md` and is not customer-visible.
