# Pricing overview

**Understanding-oriented.** This page explains *what* each CoreLink tier
includes, *why* the tier boundaries are where they are, and *how* the
bill is computed. For interactive estimation, see the
[cost calculator](./calculator). For head-to-head comparisons against
self-hosted alternatives, see [vs. alternatives](./comparison).

## The six tiers

CoreLink ships six customer-facing tiers. Four are self-serve via
Stripe Checkout (**Solo, Starter, Pro, Max**); one is instant-activation
(Free); one routes through an inquiry form (Enterprise).

**Pro is the recommended tier** — it is the best-value anchor for a team
shipping production builds.

| Tier | Monthly price | Who it's for | Included CAS storage | BYOK | Region pin | Activation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | Personal projects + evaluation | 10 GB | — | shared | Instant |
| **Solo** | $15 | The solo developer | 50 GB | — | 1 region | Stripe Checkout |
| **Starter** | $35 | A small team | 150 GB | — | 1 region | Stripe Checkout |
| **Pro** ⭐ | $50 | Best value — production teams | 500 GB | — | up to 2 regions | Stripe Checkout |
| **Max** | $149 | Scaling teams, serious volume | 2 TB | — | up to 2 regions | Stripe Checkout |
| **Enterprise** | Contact us | Multi-region / regulated / BYOK | Custom | included | all 4 regions | Inquiry form |

All tiers run on the same data plane, the same SLO catalog, and the
same audit and residency model. The differences are quotas, BYOK
inclusion, region count, and support tier — not capability.

RBAC (owner / admin / member / viewer roles) governs who can invite or
remove teammates on every tier; no tier limits how many teammates you
can invite. No self-serve tier offers BYOK — it is an Enterprise-only
capability.

## How the bill is computed

At v0.1 every tier is **hard-capped** at its included quotas — there is
no silent overage billing. The monthly invoice for a paid tier is
simply its flat base fee:

```
invoice = base_fee + optional_add_ons
```

A tenant that reaches 100% of its CAS storage quota receives a
`429 Too Many Requests` (with an upgrade CTA) until the next calendar
month resets, or until the tenant admin upgrades tier. The cap is a
feature, not a punishment — it is what keeps the price predictable and
keeps the Free tier free of charge. Metered overage is deferred to v0.2.

| Tier | Monthly base | Overage |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | hard cap — upgrade to continue |
| Solo | $15 | hard cap — upgrade to continue |
| Starter | $35 | hard cap — upgrade to continue |
| Pro | $50 | hard cap — upgrade to continue |
| Max | $149 | hard cap — upgrade to continue |
| Enterprise | Contact us | negotiated |

## Why no per-egress line item

Cloud egress is, on most managed cache vendors, the dominant line item
on a heavy-CI bill. CoreLink stores cache content on Cloudflare R2,
which charges zero for egress on reads to the public internet and to
Cloudflare's network. CoreLink passes that property through to
customers: paid tiers price CAS storage, not per-GB bytes leaving the
cache.

This is structurally why the CoreLink bill at heavy read volume is
typically a multiple smaller than the S3-backed self-hosted equivalent
at the same hit-rate and working-set. The [comparison page](./comparison)
walks the arithmetic at three scale points.

## Why no per-seat line item on the paid tiers

CoreLink does not charge per developer seat. Cache load correlates with
build volume and CI parallelism, not with seat count, and per-seat
pricing creates a perverse incentive against onboarding the next
engineer to the cache. CoreLink does not limit the number of
teammates you can invite on any tier; access is governed by
role-based permissions (owner / admin / member / viewer), not a seat
count.

## Why no compute / CPU line item

CoreLink is a cache, not a build runner. Customers run their own
runners (GitHub Actions, BuildJet, self-hosted, etc.); CoreLink serves
the cache. There is therefore no per-minute compute line item on the
CoreLink invoice — the customer is already paying their CI vendor for
compute.

## What the Free tier is for

The Free tier is designed for two cases:

1. **Solo developers** running personal monorepos. 10 GB of CAS storage
   is enough for a typical personal Bazel build with daily incremental
   green builds. (A solo dev who outgrows it graduates to the
   **Solo** tier at $15/mo.)
2. **Evaluation by larger teams** who want to wire CoreLink into a
   single CI pipeline alongside their existing cache for a two-week
   shadow comparison.

Free is not a trial that expires. It is a permanent free tier with
hard quotas. Most teams whose CI volume justifies a managed cache
graduate to Solo, Starter, or Pro within a week of seriously using it.

## What Enterprise gives you that Max does not

- **BYOK inclusion** — Enterprise is the only tier that offers it; no
  self-serve tier has a BYOK add-on.
- **All four regions** simultaneously, not just two.
- **Negotiated DPA** rather than clickwrap, including custom data-handling
  language for regulated industries (financial services, healthcare,
  defense-adjacent).
- **Negotiated cache retention** up to 730 days, versus the 365-day
  default on Max.
- **Dedicated support** with a 1-hour SLO during business hours, with
  named technical account manager assignments above a usage threshold.
- **Custom storage quotas.**

Enterprise is the right fit when at least one of the following is
true: residency requirements exceed two regions; security review
requires BYOK; the customer's CI fleet exceeds Max's quotas during
peak.

### The canonical 6-tier set

The public site and the internal billing model
(`crates/corelink-tier-selection/src/tier.rs`) share one frozen
taxonomy: **Free, Solo, Starter, Pro, Max, Enterprise**. The four paid
tiers (Solo, Starter, Pro, Max) self-serve through Stripe Checkout;
Free is instant-activation; Enterprise routes through the inquiry
form. There is no longer any held-back internal SKU — the public
surface and the code are the same six tiers.
