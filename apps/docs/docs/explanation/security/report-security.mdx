## How to report

1. Send an email to `security@humangr.com` with subject prefix
   `[VULN-REPORT]`. PGP-encrypted submission is not currently offered —
   send the report over plain email.
2. Include enough detail to reproduce: affected endpoint or component,
   request payload, expected vs observed behaviour. Proof-of-concept code
   is welcome but not required.
3. Tell us your preferred attribution (handle for the Hall of Fame at
   GA +30 days, or "anonymous").
4. Tell us your geography so we can route to the on-call rotation that
   covers your timezone.

## What you can expect

- **Acknowledgement within 1 business day** with a tracking ID
  (`VULN-YYYY-NNNN`).
- **Triage decision within 5 business days** with a provisional CVSS v3.1
  severity and in-scope/out-of-scope determination.
- Fix-or-mitigate cadence:
  - CVSS 9.0+ (Critical) — hotfix within 24 hours.
  - CVSS 7.0–8.9 (High) — hotfix within 7 days.
  - CVSS 4.0–6.9 (Medium) — patch within 30 days.
  - CVSS < 4.0 (Low) — patch within 90 days or next scheduled release.
- Public disclosure at the end of the 90-day window (or earlier by
  agreement), coordinated with a CVE assignment where applicable.
- **No legal action** against good-faith researchers who comply with this
  policy.

## Safe harbor

If you make a good-faith effort to comply with this policy we will not
pursue or support legal action against you (DMCA, CFAA, or analogous
foreign statutes) and will treat your research as authorised under
18 U.S.C. §1030. Full safe-harbor text lives in the legally-binding VDP at
`specs/_security/vulnerability-disclosure-policy.md` §6.

## Scope

**In-scope:** the live CoreLink hosts on `humangr.com`
(`corelink-api.humangr.com`, `corelink-docs.humangr.com`,
`humangr.com/corelink/*` for the admin UI), the publicly distributed
`corelink-cli` (the `humangr.com/install.sh` installer and the
`HumanGuardrail/tap/corelink` Homebrew formula), and the CoreLink Cloudflare
Worker entrypoints. The server source repository is private and no CoreLink
crates are published to a public registry, so neither is in scope.

**Out-of-scope:** third-party sub-processors (report directly to the
vendor — list at [`/trust/sub-processor-register`](/trust/sub-processor-register)),
social-engineering of staff, physical attacks, denial-of-service capability
demonstrations, findings only reachable with an out-of-date browser, and
already-disclosed third-party dependency vulnerabilities.

## Bounty program

**Pre-GA (today):** recognition only. Researchers who submit valid reports
will be acknowledged in the public Hall of Fame at GA launch +30 days
(`/security/hall-of-fame`).

**GA +30 days (planned):** monetary rewards on a USD scale (Critical
USD 2,500 – USD 10,000; High USD 1,000 – USD 2,500; Medium USD 250 –
USD 1,000; Low recognition + swag) subject to Finance sign-off, OFAC /
EU / UK sanctions screening, and Owner+Security-Lead 2-key authorisation
per ADR-0034b.
