## How to be credited

When you submit a report under our [Security Policy](/security/policy):

- Tell us **how to credit you**: full name, handle, organisation, link.
- Tell us if you want to be **anonymous** — we will still acknowledge the
  finding internally but will not list you publicly.
- Once the fix is released and the embargo lifts, we add you here within
  **5 business days**.

## Past Hall of Fame entries policy

We keep entries here **indefinitely**. If you ask to be removed (e.g.,
employer policy change, personal preference), we honour the request.
