# Sub-processors

> **DRAFT — pending Legal + Finance + Security review.** The authoritative

> file for procurement teams who cannot pull from the repository.

## Notification policy

CoreLink commits to a **30-day prior notice** before any new sub-processor
begins processing customer personal data. Notices are DKIM-signed and delivered
to the account owner contact.

## How to subscribe to change notices

1. Open a support ticket of type **"Compliance / Sub-processor Notice"**.
2. Provide the email address that should receive DKIM-signed notices.
3. We confirm subscription within TBD business days.
