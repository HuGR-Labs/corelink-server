## Recommended

**#3 — "Multi-tenant Bazel cache, TLA+ verified, BYOK-ready."**

Rationale: Product Hunt's audience is developer-leaning and rewards specificity. The tagline signals three differentiators (multi-tenancy, formal verification, BYOK) in 53 characters, which is the strongest specificity-per-character ratio of the candidates. It also avoids superlatives ("the best", "fastest") that the audience parses as marketing noise.

## Fallback

If A/B testing pre-launch indicates audience skew toward compliance framing, **#4** is the secondary choice. Compliance-forward framing tests well with regulated-sector adjacent audiences.

## Constraints respected

- ≤ 60 characters per PH convention.
- No specific dollar amounts.
- No competitor disparagement.
- No unverified compliance claims (TLA+ verification is canonically documented; BYOK-ready is canonically documented).
