# CoreLink Community Code of Conduct

## Pledge

CoreLink contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in
our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Standard

We adopt the **Contributor Covenant v2.1** verbatim as our community
standard. The canonical text is published at:

    https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

For tools and external auditors that require the full Covenant text
on disk, run:

    scripts/fetch-license-texts.sh    # also fetches CoC text

## What is enforced

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our
  mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals but for
  the overall community.

## What is NOT tolerated

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, or sexual attention or
  advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in
  a professional setting.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour
may be reported to the project maintainers at **conduct@humangr.com**.

All complaints will be:

1. Acknowledged within 24 hours.
2. Reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
3. Treated with confidentiality regarding the reporter.

The maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of
the reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good
faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by
other members of the project's leadership.

Enforcement guidelines follow the Contributor Covenant v2.1
Community Impact Ladder (Correction → Warning → Temporary Ban →
Permanent Ban). See:

    https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces and applies
when an individual is representing the community in public spaces
(GitHub, Slack, conferences, mailing lists, etc.).
