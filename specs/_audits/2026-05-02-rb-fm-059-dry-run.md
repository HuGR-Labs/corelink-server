## 3. Findings

- **DO actor model `Mutex<()>` decision_lock canonical:** WI-S07-003
  ships per-instance `Mutex<()>` mirroring the DO actor model so
  `prop_quota_atomic_no_race` asserts no two concurrent QuotaCheck
  invocations can write inconsistent reservation state. F-001 closure
  preserved (no global mutable state).
- **Size-proportional reservation TTL canonical:** the formula
  `min(7d, max(60s, req_bytes/1MB/s × 2))` (Lote 10.7bis R5 P0-2)
  ensures multipart 160 GiB does NOT over-quota mid-upload. The
  reservation tracker auto-releases pending reservations after the
  size-proportional TTL expires, restoring `bytes_used` budget.
- **Tenant isolation strict per CTRL-ISO-005:** the prop suite at 10k
  iter never observes a cross-tenant reservation lookup; the existence-
  oracle attack surface is closed by the typed `TenantId` newtype +
  per-tenant DO sharding.
- **Audit fail-closed envelope:** every QuotaDecision arm
  (Allow/Deny429/Reserve) emits its audit event BEFORE state
  mutation; emit failure preserves the prior state and surfaces
  `QuotaError::AuditEmissionFailed`. PROVISIONAL transitional 429 +
  Retry-After per ADR-0020 FROZEN until S-08 rate-limit DO ships.
- **Reservation expiry as FM-059 indicator:** the dashboard
  `Dedup_ReservationExpirySpike` SEV-2 alert + the SLO probe
  `prop_check_duration_under_3ms_p99` together pin the canonical SLO
  envelope; sustained drift signals the DO singleton is approaching
  the 50 GB hard limit.
