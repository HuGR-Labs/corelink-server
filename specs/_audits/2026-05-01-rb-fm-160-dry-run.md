# RB-FM-160 host-side dry-run — Auth Invalid Storm

> **Sprint:** S-03 (Auth Real) · **WI:** WI-S03-008 §6.1.4
> **Runbook:** `specs/05_quality/runbooks/RB-FM-160-auth-invalid-storm.md`
> **Harness:** `scripts/rb_fm_160_dry_run.sh`
> **Date:** 2026-05-01 · **Mode:** host-side simulation (staging dry-run deferred)

## 1. Scope

Host-side dry-run validates the runbook step ordering + the load-bearing cross-component invariants the on-call engineer would exercise during a real auth-invalid-storm incident. Does NOT page real PagerDuty / hit real Cloudflare WAF / drive 1000 req/s × 30 min controlled brute force — those land in the staging dry-run when the CF account is provisioned (see §5 forward).

Steps validated (per RB-FM-160 + WI-S03-008 §6.1.4 + §9.5):

1. **Detection** — Tower middleware property test (`prop_auth_middleware::prop_malformed_token_rejected`) asserts every malformed-token shape surfaces `COR_AUTH_INVALID_TOKEN` AND the inner handler is NEVER reached on a malformed-token arm.
2. **Communication** — SEV-2 page synthesis stub; payload shape matches `RB-FM-160 § Comunicação`.
3. **Mitigação imediata** — attack profile identification + pattern detection + per-pattern mitigation routing + customer notification triggers.
4. **Diagnóstico** — revocation orchestrator idempotency property test (`prop_revocation::revoke_is_idempotent_across_retries`) at 10k iter green — audit_outbox + audit chain emit semantics hold under N-replay.
5. **Resolução** — cross-component revocation+audit gate (`prop_auth_full::prop_revocation_race_full_stack`) at 10k iter green — full-stack contract that the production stack honours.
6. **Post-incident** — post-mortem template scaffold; threat intel feed update.
7. **Drift detection** — runbook canonical headers all present (`Detecção`, `Comunicação`, `Mitigação imediata`, `Diagnóstico`, `Resolução`, `Post-incident`, `References`).
