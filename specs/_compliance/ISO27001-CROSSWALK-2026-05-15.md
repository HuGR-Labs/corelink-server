### 1.1 Legend

- **Status (per row):**
  - `Implemented (I)` — control is in place; evidence already produced (auto or manual) for SOC 2.
  - `Partial (P)` — design exists but evidence-of-operation or scope is incomplete; closes alongside SOC 2 GAP-XX remediation.
  - `Gap (G)` — no equivalent control; ISO-unique work required (see `ISO27001-GAP-ANALYSIS.md`).
  - `N/A` — control not applicable to CoreLink scope (justify in SoA; typically physical / outsourced dev).

- **SOC 2 overlap:** of the 90 in-scope controls, **82 (91%)** are already evidenced via SOC 2 TSC criteria (CC1..CC9 + A1 + C1 + PI1 + Privacy); only **8 (9%)** require ISO-unique work (see §6.3 + `ISO27001-GAP-ANALYSIS.md`).

### 6.3 ISO-unique gaps (not covered by SOC 2 evidence)

These are surfaced in `specs/_compliance/ISO27001-GAP-ANALYSIS.md` with detail:

| ID | Annex A ref | Title | Severity | Reason ISO-unique |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GAP-ISO-01 | A.5.9 | Formal asset register (vs informal asset inventory) | minor | SOC 2 doesn't require an enumerated asset register; ISO 27002 §5.9 does (assets + owners + classification) |
| GAP-ISO-02 | A.5.10 | Acceptable Use Policy formalized as standalone doc | minor | SOC 2 covers via code-of-conduct embedded in README; ISO expects standalone signed AUP |
| GAP-ISO-03 | A.5.20 | Supplier SLA contract review depth (security clauses) | minor | SOC 2 CC9.2 stops at DPA + termination clauses; ISO 27002 §5.20 expects per-contract security requirements review |
| GAP-ISO-04 | A.5.30 · A.8.14 | BCP testing depth (multi-scenario business-impact analysis) | minor | SOC 2 A1.3 satisfied by DR drills; ISO 27031 §8.4 (referenced by A.5.30) expects formal BIA + RTO/RPO per business process |
| GAP-ISO-05 | A.6.5 | Offboarding checklist (employee + contractor + advisor) | minor | SOC 2 covered implicitly via Clerk de-provisioning; ISO expects signed checklist with all asset returns + access revocations |
| GAP-ISO-06 | A.7.7 | Clear-desk and clear-screen policy formalized | minor | Not surfaced by SOC 2 (remote-first); ISO A.7.7 expects documented policy even for remote workers |
| GAP-ISO-07 | A.7.9 · A.8.1 | MDM (Mobile Device Management) for workstations | minor | SOC 2 covers via Clerk MFA-bound sessions; ISO A.8.1 + A.7.9 expect inventoried managed endpoints with remote-wipe |
| GAP-ISO-08 | A.5.34 (PIMS overlap) | ISO 27701 PIMS Annex A.5.36-style controls (privacy by design specifically) | informational | SOC 2 Privacy + LGPD/GDPR cover the substance; ISO 27701 (privacy extension) controls are formally separate; in-scope **only** if pursuing 27701 — informational for 27001 cert |

**ISO-unique gap count:** **7 active gaps** for ISO 27001:2022 cert (GAP-ISO-01..GAP-ISO-07) + **1 informational** (GAP-ISO-08 PIMS, deferred to 2028 27701 track).

> **The 91% SOC-2-overlap headline is conservative.** Most "Partial" rows in §2–§5 share remediation work with an existing SOC 2 GAP-XX (e.g., GAP-01 quarterly access review automation closes A.5.18 + CC6.3 simultaneously). Once the 33-GAP SOC 2 register clears, only the 7 ISO-unique gaps remain — that's the actual incremental work for ISO 27001 certification on top of SOC 2 Type I.
