## 7. Refresh + governance

- **Quarterly refresh:** triggered by `LGPD-DPO-MONTHLY-CHECKLIST.md` item 16 (added GAP-22-followup). DPO reviews RoPA rows for drift; bump version and re-attest if drift detected.
