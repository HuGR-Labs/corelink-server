# RB-DSR-TICKET-TRIAGE — DSR Inbound Ticket Triage

> **Purpose:** map inbound DSR (Data Subject Request) requests through the **existing DSR pipeline** (`crates/corelink-dsr/`) end-to-end: intake → identity verification → routing → fulfilment → legal escalation when unusual.
> **Audience:** Support T1 + T2 agents (intake), DPO (decisions), Legal-on-call (escalations).
> **Parent:** `specs/_runbooks/RB-CUSTOMER-SUPPORT-T-90.md` (general support).
> **DSR pipeline canonical sources:** `crates/corelink-dsr/` (API + state machine), `specs/05_quality/runbooks/RB-DSR-INTAKE-FAILURE.md`, `specs/05_quality/runbooks/RB-DSR-ERASURE-INCOMPLETE.md`, `specs/04_sprints/S11/work_items/WI-S11-001-dsr-api-7-endpoints-jwt-receipt-mfa-step-up.md`, `specs/04_sprints/S16/work_items/WI-S16-004-dsr-self-service-form-6-direitos-mfa-jwt-receipt.md`, `specs/tla/dsr_erasure_atomicity.tla` (TLA+ proof).
> **Regulatory anchors:** LGPD Arts. 18, 19, 48 (Brazil); GDPR Arts. 12, 15–22, 33 (EU); state privacy laws (US — CCPA/CPRA, VCDPA, CPA, etc.).
> **Hard rule:** **30-day clock is wall-clock from receipt, not from verification.** Identity verification delay does not pause the clock.

---

## 1. Scope

### In-scope DSR right types (the "6 direitos" per WI-S16-004)

| Right | LGPD article | GDPR article | Pipeline endpoint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Access | Art. 18.II | Art. 15 | `POST /dsr/access` |
| Rectification | Art. 18.III | Art. 16 | `POST /dsr/rectification` |
| Erasure | Art. 18.VI | Art. 17 | `POST /dsr/erasure` |
| Restriction | Art. 18.IV | Art. 18 | `POST /dsr/restriction` |
| Portability | Art. 18.V | Art. 20 | `POST /dsr/portability` |
| Objection | Art. 18.IV | Art. 21 | `POST /dsr/objection` |

### Out-of-scope
- **Anonymous-data requests** — not subject to LGPD/GDPR (clarify with requester).
- **Account-management** that doesn't invoke a DSR right (use general support tracks per `RB-CUSTOMER-SUPPORT-T-90.md`).
- **DPA copy requests** — handled as P3 in general support; not a DSR.
- **Subpoenas / law-enforcement requests** — direct to Legal, do NOT enter DSR pipeline.

## 2. Intake channels

| Channel | Path | Auto-DSR? |
|---|---|---|
| `dsr@humangr.com` | Support queue → DSR triage tag | yes, after agent confirms intent |
| In-app DSR form (per WI-S16-004) | `/dsr/request` → backend API directly | yes, automatic |
| CLI: `corelink dsr request --type {right_type}` | DSR API direct | yes, automatic |
| Inbound to `support@` mentioning GDPR/LGPD rights keywords | Support queue → auto-route per RB-CUSTOMER-SUPPORT-T-90 §3.2 | semi (agent confirms) |
| Letter mail (rare; pre-GA fallback for non-tech requesters) | Scan → manual upload to DSR API | manual |

### 2.1 Intake hard rules
1. **Receipt timestamp** (`{{received_at}}`) is captured at the **earliest** channel touchpoint (form submission, API call, email received-at). NOT at agent-acknowledgment.
2. **Identity not yet verified** does NOT delay receipt recording. Verification follows in §4.
3. **Auto-acknowledgment** template `SR-DSR-ACK` (per `SUPPORT-RESPONSE-TEMPLATES.md` #10) fires within 24h.
4. **JWT receipt** is generated by `corelink-dsr` at intake per WI-S11-001 §3 — customer can present this receipt to verify their request is in-pipeline.

## 3. Triage decision tree

```
DSR REQUEST RECEIVED
       │
       ▼
Q1: Is the requester the data subject themselves OR
    an authorized agent / parent of a minor / legal
    representative?
       │
       ├── Data subject themselves ──▶ Standard verification path (§4)
       │
       ├── Authorized agent ──▶ Verification + agency proof (§4.2)
       │
       └── Unclear / can't tell ──▶ Reply requesting clarification;
                                   30d clock STILL RUNNING; document
                                   the ambiguity in ticket audit log
       │
       ▼
Q2: Is the right one of the 6 supported (Access,
    Rectification, Erasure, Restriction, Portability,
    Objection)?
       │
       ├── YES ──▶ Route to pipeline endpoint per §5
       │
       └── NO / unclear ──▶ Reply asking which right(s); 30d clock
                            STILL RUNNING; common confusions: "delete"
                            (Erasure), "give me my data" (Access vs
                            Portability — clarify), "stop using" (Restriction
                            vs Objection — clarify)
       │
       ▼
Q3: Is the request UNUSUAL? (See §6 unusual-request flags)
       │
       ├── YES ──▶ Pause auto-routing; LEGAL ESCALATION per §7;
       │           do NOT execute pipeline action without Legal sign-off
       │
       └── NO ──▶ Execute standard fulfillment path (§5) under
                  DPO supervision
```

## 4. Identity verification

### 4.1 Standard verification (data subject themselves)

Per WI-S11-001 §4 + WI-S16-004 §3 (MFA step-up):

1. **Account-linked email match** — DSR request must originate from an email on file for the tenant OR include a verification challenge sent to the account email.
2. **MFA step-up** — if the customer has MFA enabled, a step-up challenge is sent via authenticator app / SMS.
3. **JWT receipt** — generated by `corelink-dsr` and returned to the customer; subsequent status checks require this receipt.
4. **Verification timeout** — if the customer doesn't complete verification within 30 days from receipt, the request is closed as `pending-customer-verification`; the 30d regulatory clock has expired. We notify them once at 15d and again at 25d.

### 4.2 Authorized-agent verification

Additional requirements (per GDPR Art. 12 + LGPD Art. 18 §3):
- **Signed written authorization** from the data subject (scanned PDF acceptable).
- **Government-issued ID** of both the data subject and the agent (data subject's only, redacted to confirm identity match).
- **Notarized** if the request involves Erasure across an enterprise account (we won't erase enterprise data on agent's word alone).
- **DPO review** required before fulfillment.

### 4.3 Minors / legal-rep verification

- **Birth certificate** or equivalent proving relationship.
- **DPO + Legal joint review** before any fulfillment, even Access.
- US: COPPA considerations if data subject is under 13.
- Brazil: LGPD Art. 14 (children's data) — additional consent verification.

## 5. Pipeline routing per right type

Each right type routes to the existing `corelink-dsr` endpoint with specific runbook overlays.

### 5.1 Access (`POST /dsr/access`)

- Triggers data-export aggregation across: account records, audit-chain references, BYOK envelope references, metering records.
- Output: portable JSON bundle delivered via signed URL (7-day expiry) per WI-S11-001 §5.
- Standard timeline: 5 business days; max 30 days regulatory.
- Failure mode: `RB-DSR-INTAKE-FAILURE.md` if backend can't enumerate sources.

### 5.2 Rectification (`POST /dsr/rectification`)

- Customer specifies field + correct value.
- Pipeline updates production records + writes audit-chain entry referencing old/new values.
- Standard timeline: 2 business days; max 30 days regulatory.
- Caveat: audit-chain entries themselves are **immutable** (we rectify by appending a correction event, not overwriting).

### 5.3 Erasure (`POST /dsr/erasure`)

- The HARDEST right — strict consistency required per TLA+ proof `dsr_erasure_atomicity.tla`.
- Triggers atomic erasure across: account, cached blobs, BYOK envelopes (pseudonymized; we don't have key material), metering records.
- Audit-chain entries are anonymized (regulatory retention applies; customer identifier replaced with pseudonymous token per `RB-DSR-ERASURE-INCOMPLETE.md`).
- Cached blob retention: evicted within 24h of erasure confirmation.
- Standard timeline: 5 business days; max 30 days regulatory.
- Failure mode: `RB-DSR-ERASURE-INCOMPLETE.md` — CRITICAL severity, INV-DATA-ERASURE-COMPLETE invariant.

### 5.4 Restriction (`POST /dsr/restriction`)

- Marks account as "restricted-processing"; pipeline blocks all non-storage operations.
- Read access preserved; no writes, no analytics aggregation, no metering for billing purposes (special billing handling).
- Standard timeline: 2 business days; max 30 days regulatory.

### 5.5 Portability (`POST /dsr/portability`)

- Similar to Access but specifically machine-readable + transferable to another controller.
- Output: structured JSON per a documented schema (we publish `specs/_legal/dsr-portability-schema.md` — TBD pre-GA).
- Standard timeline: 5 business days; max 30 days regulatory.

### 5.6 Objection (`POST /dsr/objection`)

- Customer specifies purpose objected to (analytics, marketing, profiling).
- Pipeline updates processing-purpose flags; some purposes can be globally disabled (marketing), others require case-by-case legal-basis analysis (e.g., legitimate-interest balancing per GDPR Art. 21).
- DPO review required if objection is to a legitimate-interest-based processing.
- Standard timeline: 5 business days; max 30 days regulatory.

## 6. Unusual-request flags (DPO + Legal escalation triggers)

Any of the following marks a request as **unusual** — pipeline auto-execution paused, DPO + Legal review mandatory:

| Flag | Why it's unusual | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Request scope crosses tenants (e.g., "erase my data from ALL CoreLink customers") | We are processor, not controller; not our right to action | Legal drafts response explaining controller-processor split per GDPR Art. 28 |
| Request from a public figure / journalist / known activist (matched against internal watchlist) | High litigation / press risk if mishandled | Legal owns response wording end-to-end |
| Request volume from one requester ≥ 3 in 30 days | Potential abuse / DDoS-via-DSR | DPO may invoke GDPR Art. 12(5) "manifestly unfounded or excessive" — written justification + fee or refusal |
| Request involves data also subject to regulatory retention (financial records, audit logs, KYC) | Erasure conflicts with retention obligations | Legal identifies retention basis; partial fulfillment with reasoned response |
| Request comes during active litigation / legal hold involving the requester | Erasure / rectification may constitute spoliation | Legal puts hold on DSR; reasoned response explaining hold |
| Request includes threats / coercive language | Possible duress or fraudulent intent | DPO + Legal joint review before any action; consider Verification escalation |
| Request from a jurisdiction not previously seen (no data-transfer assessment on file) | May require additional Schrems II analysis or local-law review | Legal flags; may extend regulatory clock per GDPR Art. 12(3) |
| Erasure request from an account with open Stripe disputes | Erasure pre-dispute resolution may impede our defense | Legal + Finance joint hold; reasoned response |
| Identity verification fails repeatedly but requester persists | Possible impersonation attempt | Security escalation; preserve attempts for incident analysis |
| Request invokes a right not yet supported (e.g., automated-decision-making contestation under GDPR Art. 22) | We don't process for ADM; response is "not applicable to our processing" but Legal-cleared | Legal owns the response |

### 6.1 Unusual-request handling protocol
1. **Mark ticket** `dsr:unusual:flag:{flag_id}`.
2. **Pause pipeline auto-execution** — no `POST /dsr/{right}` until DPO sign-off.
3. **DPO review within 5 business days** of unusual flag.
4. **Legal escalation within 5 business days** if DPO defers (§7).
5. **Customer notified** within 5 business days that we're applying extended-review process per GDPR Art. 12(3) / LGPD Art. 19 (up to 60-day extension).

## 7. Legal escalation path

```
DSR request flagged UNUSUAL
       │
       ▼
[T+0..T+24h] Support agent tags ticket dsr:unusual; pipeline pauses
       │
       ▼
[T+24h..T+5d] DPO reviews flag, assesses applicable law
       │
       ├── DPO sign-off "proceed standard" ──▶ Resume pipeline (§5)
       │
       ├── DPO sign-off "proceed with modification" ──▶ Pipeline with
       │   overlay (e.g., partial erasure preserving regulatory retention)
       │
       └── DPO defers to Legal ──▶ Step T+5d below
       │
       ▼
[T+5d..T+10d] Legal-on-call reviews; outside counsel consulted if needed
       │
       ├── Legal sign-off "proceed" ──▶ Resume pipeline
       │
       ├── Legal sign-off "extend response window per GDPR Art. 12(3)" ──▶
       │   Customer notified of extension (60 additional days max)
       │
       ├── Legal sign-off "refuse — manifestly unfounded or excessive" ──▶
       │   Customer notified with reasoned response + right-to-complain
       │   to supervisory authority
       │
       └── Legal sign-off "partial — split fulfillment" ──▶ Some rights
           fulfilled, others held with explanation
       │
       ▼
Fulfillment OR reasoned refusal communicated to customer via
SR-DSR-COMPLETED template (per SUPPORT-RESPONSE-TEMPLATES.md #11) with
right-type-specific block + extension/refusal block as applicable.
```

### 7.1 Legal-escalation hard rules
1. **Documenting refusals** — every refusal includes the legal basis (GDPR Art. 12(5), retention obligation, etc.) and reference to right-to-complain. No bare "no".
2. **Outside counsel** — engaged for novel-jurisdiction questions, public-figure requests, and any erasure crossing into active litigation. Legal-on-call decides.
3. **Audit trail** — every escalation step is logged in `specs/_audits/dsr-escalations/DSR-{{dsr_id}}.md` (DPO writes; Legal countersigns).
4. **Regulator-coordination** — if a request is paired with a regulator inquiry (ANPD, lead EU DPA), Legal coordinates the dual response.
